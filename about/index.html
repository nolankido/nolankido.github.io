Perspective

I am interested in how better tools, better information, and better reasoning can improve the way people make decisions and create things.

My interests cross technology, artificial intelligence, business, analytical problem-solving, media, and creative systems. I am especially drawn to projects that require learning quickly, combining ideas from different fields, and turning complexity into something practical.

I prefer experimentation to rigid categories. Some interests become projects, some become writing, and some remain private. This website is meant to provide a durable public point of reference without attempting to document everything I do.
