From reading to making

Choose the kind of piece.

Three practical routes through material already on the site. These are approaches to try, not a portfolio of completed personal projects.

Writing / A first version

Turn a rough idea into something you can share.

Choose a reader, a small scope, and a finish line. Use optional AI assistance without letting it replace the idea you wanted to express.

Work toward: one small version, rather than a growing list of possibilities.

Visual storytelling / A poker-specific guide

From hand note to poker vlog

Follow a poker-specific workflow from recorded action to narration, graphics, and a companion page. Separate what happened from later interpretation.

Work toward: a coherent explanation built from supportable facts. This is a guide, not a published vlog episode.

Personal stories / Someone's voice

Preserve a family story without losing the person's voice.

Start with permission, listen to the speaker, and let them review what you keep. The technology should serve the story.

Work toward: a version the speaker has had a chance to review, not a polished replacement for their voice.
