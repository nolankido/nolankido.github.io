Latency / Effort / Cost

Nineteen seconds of elapsed processing is not nineteen seconds saved.

The table records case elapsed time around generation and application checks. Model-response receipts have their own durations. Neither measures the person's work preparing a review, checking the draft, correcting it, approving it, or maintaining the setup.

For a whole-task comparison, measure those activities and the usual method at an acceptable common quality. Record waiting separately; it counts as occupied time only when it prevents other useful work. The time calculator explains this accounting. Missing preparation, review, or baseline measurements must remain unknown, rather than being entered as zero.

These records also lack measured hardware, electricity, setup, and human-review costs. They cannot establish a total cost or net saving. There is no matched hosted-model run of these same reviews in the inspected evidence.
