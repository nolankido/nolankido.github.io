Practical AI / Human judgment

Useful AI starts with a life, not a tool.

I work with AI to build tools, develop writing, and make difficult ideas easier to work with. The interesting question is not how much it can generate. It is whether the result is useful, trustworthy enough for its purpose, and worth the effort it takes to use.

This section connects the technical checks with the everyday reasons for doing the work. Build a brief for one task, read the argument behind it, or follow a practical guide. The tools and recorded case below are working starting points, not promises of what AI can do.
