# Living Well: technology and actual-life utility

Review date: October 9, 2026.
Base release: `66c507d5149b81ee666eb48f2bd2f3a94e7fbb01`.

## Editorial assessment

Living Well already provides an inviting philosophical foundation, 23 published readings, three proposed experiments, five worksheets, and 66 resource selections in twelve collections. The practical gap is not a shortage of tools. It is the distance between discovering a resource and completing a task with it.

The weekly-reset and AI-learning guides contain helpful illustrative examples. Most other guides explain principles and boundaries without showing a complete input, output, verification step, and finished result. The resource library is broad, but it is organized mainly by subject and provider rather than by an outcome a visitor needs today. Neither issue calls for replacing the existing spiritual, philosophical, creative, or contemplative content.

The strongest extension is a practical reading path called Technology for Real Life. It should connect useful examples with the human purposes behind them: less avoidable administration, easier participation, clearer coordination, creative expression, thoughtful reflection, and retained judgment.

## Planned implementation in this branch

Add eight published readings to the existing editorial catalog: one Technology for Real Life practice path and seven worked guides. Use the existing rendering, source-note, related-reading, date, RSS, topic, and guide-navigation mechanisms rather than introducing a separate application or another top-level website brand.

The seven guides address scattered notes, shared plans, finding ordinary documents, turning a rough idea into something shareable, making digital reading easier, reflection without outsourcing judgment, and evaluating whether a tool actually helped. Every guide should include an adequate no-AI route, a bounded technology contribution, a plainly labeled fictional example where applicable, and a concrete way to check or finish the task.

Maintain the existing 66-resource library rather than inflate it with near-duplicate product recommendations. Link new guides to the most relevant existing collections and to current official instructions where a product feature is described. Free access to Living Well does not imply free devices, unlimited cloud storage, or free access to every optional AI capability.

## Reader and public-message principles

The intended message is that Nolan is interested in what technology makes possible in ordinary life, not only in impressive demonstrations. The section should convey curiosity, competence, consideration for other people, and willingness to compare a tool with a simpler alternative. These are editorial aims, not claims about reader perceptions or credentials.

Do not manufacture personal use, saved-time measurements, spiritual beliefs, product endorsements, private conversations, or completed field trials. A fictional worked example is an example, not a successful AI test. Product documentation can support the description of a feature; it cannot establish that a workflow improves wellbeing or has been tested on every device.

Keep spirituality present on its own terms. The reflection guide should support a person's own questions and choices, not instruct a model to reveal hidden meaning, diagnose them, or provide spiritual authority. Practical utility also includes accessibility and ongoing support; needing a useful tool regularly is not failure.

## Future firsthand publishing

A future field note can grow from any guide, but must start with an actual supplied account. Record the situation, permission to use any material, device and service versions, the original method, the change, setup and checking effort, what happened, what did not happen, and what was retained or abandoned. Keep observations from one person separate from general causal claims.

Publishing more often is not the goal. An honest example with a usable result is more valuable than another generic list of prompts. Keep the site's existing privacy posture and do not add visitor journaling forms, uploads, tracking events, or a life-coach chatbot.

## Verification requirement

This review describes the scope being implemented, not completed verification. Source and generated files must agree; all previous public routes and worksheet downloads must remain. New readings must be reachable through the existing guide and topic indexes, have valid source and related-reading links, and remain readable without JavaScript. Full retained site checks and actual deployment verification are required before reporting this release live.

## Recovery and deeper integration, October 10, 2026

The interrupted branch had not been published. Its original build and unit suite passed, but the browser suite timed out while opening a resource disclosure following no-JavaScript navigation. The recovery uses an exact exported current-branch tree rather than the older archive referenced in the first preparation. The test now explicitly waits for the destination document and confirms the target is in view before the normal pointer click; pointer testing is retained, not forced or skipped.

The next editorial gap was that each guide's outcome was only internal metadata. The recovery makes inputs, outputs, the simplest approach and completion criteria visible above the prose. Seven original plain-text templates provide a usable takeaway directly in HTML, without requiring download, new software or disclosure of personal answers. A small validated renderer ensures these are escaped text and prevents worked-guide metadata from silently claiming firsthand results.

Technology for Real Life now has a prominent entry on the Living Well homepage, its own group of seven guides within Try Something, and contextual links from four existing resource collections. The larger spiritual and creative library remains intact. The prior 26 source records and all 66 resource records are preserved, not rewritten to fit the new emphasis.

The internal TECHNOLOGY_FOR_REAL_LIFE_PUBLISHING.md defines a repeatable article/video method for genuine future contributions, distinguishing worked guides, recorded demonstrations, firsthand observations and research explanations. It is not a collection of fabricated field reports or a promised schedule. The actual release date for the eight previously unpublished pieces is October 10, 2026; product-source checks were renewed on that date.
