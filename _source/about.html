My interests connect technology, thoughtful living, tournament poker, and creative work. I like the point where a difficult idea becomes understandable, a rough version becomes useful, or a familiar question needs another look.

Ideas into useful tools.

Building a tool and explaining an idea ask different things of you. One has to work for an actual task. The other has to make sense to someone who does not already share your assumptions. The technology section starts with that connection.

Meaning, attention, and everyday life.

Living Well explores spirituality, practical wisdom, and the role technology can play in ordinary life. The free resource library offers ways to follow a question, learn something useful, or encounter a different perspective.

At the table and away from it.

Tournament poker brings play and study together. The poker section is a place for the decisions behind the hands and the work of reviewing them, with room for visual storytelling as well as analysis.

Ideas into expression.

Creative Work brings together writing, design, and visual storytelling. The broader Notes collection and a few practical resources connect the subjects.

This site is a selected introduction to my interests, with reading and resources to explore at your own pace.

For introductions, there is a short biography. For a question, a different perspective, or a simple hello, get in touch.
