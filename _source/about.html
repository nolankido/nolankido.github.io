Perspective

${intro}

My interests sit where technology, decision-making, and creative work meet. I am drawn to questions that require more than a quick answer: how a tool works, what makes a result trustworthy, or how an unfamiliar idea can become something practical.

Artificial intelligence is one part of that curiosity. I am interested in what it makes possible, but also in the judgment needed to use it well. A useful system should do more than produce an impressive demonstration. Its assumptions, limitations, and behavior deserve attention too.

The same interest carries into decisions and learning. I value clear explanations, evidence that can be checked, and the willingness to revise an approach. Starting with the basics is not a distraction from ambitious work. It is often where the useful work begins.

There is a creative side to this as well. Writing, visual storytelling, and design offer different ways to make an idea understandable. Exploring a new subject or an unfamiliar place can provide a fresh perspective on something that once seemed settled.

I do not see these as entirely separate interests. Learning can lead to a tool, a tool can make room for creative work, and trying to explain an idea can reveal what still needs to be understood.

This site brings together a little of that perspective through occasional notes on tools, decisions, and learning. It is a place to share a useful idea and leave room for a good conversation.
