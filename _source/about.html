The person, not just the topics

I like making things useful, understanding difficult ideas, and leaving room for a life beyond the work.

AI is part of how I build, write, and explore ideas. What interests me is not just what a tool can produce, but whether the result helps with something that matters. I want technology to make more room for life, not give every part of it another dashboard.

That is the connection between the different parts of this site. Practical technology, thoughtful living, tournament poker, and creative work ask different questions, but all involve attention, judgment, and the willingness to learn.

What matters to me in the work

Usefulness: start with a real purpose, not a demonstration in search of one. Clarity: give the reader enough context to understand the choice. Judgment: be willing to question a polished result, change direction, or stop when the work has done its job.

Those are priorities, not a claim that every attempt succeeds. Let AI do more. Keep the judgment. develops the distinction behind them.

What I spend my attention on

Technology is where I explore useful AI and software: starting with an actual need, checking what comes back, and knowing what still needs a person's judgment.

Living Well leaves space for meaning, spirituality, relationships, and the texture of ordinary life. Some questions deserve exploration rather than an efficient answer. Its free resource library offers ways to follow those questions.

Tournament poker combines play, uncertainty, and study. The poker section makes room for understanding the game and reviewing decisions, without confusing a good result with a good decision.

Writing and visual storytelling are another way of working through an idea. I care about what the reader or viewer can understand and feel, not just what a tool makes possible.

Outside the categories

Food, travel, and the experience of learning something unfamiliar belong in the picture too. Not every interest needs to become a project or a public update.

I keep this site focused on the ideas and material that are useful to share. Some work and much of ordinary life belong outside a public website.
