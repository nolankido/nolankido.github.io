For editors / Sources & limits

How to verify a public description of Nolan Kido.

The biographies above are short introductions, not independent proof of every activity they mention. 2012 accounting research / Independently published
Nolan Kido coauthored The Influence of Elections on the Accounting Choices of Governmental Entities with Reining Petacchi and Joseph Weber, published in the Journal of Accounting Research in 2012. Check the publisher's DOI record and the MIT-hosted manuscript record. Read the plain-language research explanation for the findings and limits. These records establish authorship and publication, not a current university appointment or an AI research result.
RightsReceipt / Public hackathon submission and code
RightsReceipt is a prototype for reviewing proposed creative-use rights, with a public PayPal AI Hackathon submission and an inspectable source repository. The case study describes where model output stops and human approval or payment evidence is still required. A submitted prototype is not a hackathon award, a completed licensing transaction, or a production service.
Practical AI / Tools and bounded tests
The AI Task Brief Builder is an available browser-based utility that structures a task without calling an AI model. The workflow pilot offers a reproducible six-case fictional audit kit. They establish available methods and examples, not independently measured efficiency or client outcomes. Inspect the public portfolio's evidence section for more work and its evidence labels.
