What you are reading

The labels matter.

Essays explore an idea. Practical guides offer an approach to try. Worked examples and fictional poker hands explain a method; they are not personal case studies. Resource directories point to other people's work, not work I am claiming as my own.

AI assistance is disclosed on the relevant articles. Proposed experiments in Field notes are plans, not completed results. The point is to make each piece useful without claiming more than it shows.
