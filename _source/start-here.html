Source and limits / How to read this site

What kind of evidence is this?

My work appears in different forms. A functioning tool, published research, a recorded AI-assisted task, a poker video, and an essay answer different questions. Use the original material and the stated limits to decide what a link can actually establish.

  • Working browser tool: Full-workflow time calculator. You can run a local comparison of preparation, AI work, review and upkeep with your own numbers. It makes no model call and cannot establish actual time savings without real observations.
  • Public software and demonstration: Review Reply Writer and Preflight. Their cases show app designs, archived examples, source links, and known mistakes. A demonstration does not establish current production quality, client adoption, or business results.
  • Recorded AI-assisted task: Eight notes to an action checklist. The original input, instruction, eleven output rows, and a limited offline check can be compared. This does not authenticate the starting claims, prove task completion, or measure productivity.
  • Peer-reviewed scholarship: 2012 accounting paper. Publication supports coauthorship in the Journal of Accounting Research. It does not establish an AI benchmark, an MIT appointment, or a current professional title.
  • Tournament records and first-person video: Poker player profile and public episodes. Independent event results, when linked, are distinguished from Nolan's own narrated tournament accounts. Gross cashes are not net profit; a video does not verify every hand detail or confer an official creator role.
  • Essay or viewpoint: Let AI do more. Keep the judgment.. This publishes an argument for keeping decisions with people. It is not a controlled study, professional endorsement, or measured outcome.
  • Proposed exercise: Living Well field notes. The material offers ideas and reflection exercises to consider, not a completed personal experiment, therapeutic result, or evidence of visitor satisfaction.

The useful question is not whether an example sounds impressive, but whether the evidence matches the claim. For the person's broader background, see About Nolan Kido. Help make these guides easier to use

If you are an adult new to this site, you can help me find unclear steps. I am inviting interest in an optional 25-minute usability session using two fictional tasks and your usual device. You may decline a topic, skip a task, or stop. No personal accounts or payments are part of the exercises.

To express interest, use the existing contact form. When scripts work, the optional context field starts with "Website usability study"; you can edit or clear it. Write your own message. Nothing is sent automatically. Please do not send task answers, completed worksheets, private records, or sensitive details. An inquiry is not consent to participate or be recorded.
