The quick-start explanation

Read the situationFollow the actionUseful termsRead the review

Read the situation first.

Look for the game, the positions, the blinds and antes, and the relevant stacks. A number followed by BB is measured in big blinds, not dollars. If the big blind is 1,000 chips, a 30,000-chip stack is 30 BB: 30,000 ÷ 1,000 = 30.

Effective stack describes the amount the relevant players can play for. With two players, stacks of 30 BB and 50 BB mean 30 BB effective at the start. Multiway hands can involve different effective stacks and side pots; a single number does not tell that whole story.

Position labels tell you where players sit relative to the dealer button. In a six-handed hand, the button, small blind, and big blind are often abbreviated BTN, SB, and BB. Other labels include under the gun (UTG), hijack (HJ), and cutoff (CO). A label such as BB can therefore describe a seat or a unit; read it in context.

Follow the action, one street at a time.

Preflop is before community cards are dealt. The flop brings three community cards, followed by one on the turn and one on the river. A reconstructed account is a way of showing the sequence, not proof that every detail was captured live.

Watch for the difference between a new total and an additional amount. In the fictional hand-review guide, a player bets 2 BB and faces a raise to 7 BB. Calling requires another 5 BB, not another 7. “Raises by” would describe an increment instead. A clear account should state which is meant.

Written card notation may use As for ace of spades, Qh for queen of hearts, Td for ten of diamonds, and 8c for eight of clubs. The cards in the hand-review guide also have a full text description.

A few terms worth knowing.

Pot
The chips or money being contested in the hand. Read whether a displayed amount is before or after the latest action.
Check
Pass the action without betting when there is no outstanding amount to call.
Call
Match the outstanding wager, subject to the chips you have available.
Raise / Three-bet
A raise increases the current wager. A three-bet is a reraise; preflop, the big blind counts as the first bet for this terminology. It does not mean “three times the size.”
Range
The set of possible hands assigned to a player. In a review, this is usually an assumption or model, not knowledge of the hidden cards.
Ante
A forced contribution to the pot separate from the blinds. Record the actual ante arrangement rather than assuming one is present.
Bubble
The point just before the remaining players reach a payout threshold, commonly the first paid positions.
Effective stack
For two players, the smaller relevant stack, which limits the amount they can contest against each other.

Keep three kinds of information separate.

The account: what the recorded or reconstructed action says. An approximate stack or uncertain suit should stay labeled as uncertain.

The thinking at the table: the player's explanation of the choice, as honestly remembered. It is not a transcript unless it was actually recorded.

The later review: a new interpretation using additional time, evidence, or tools. It should identify assumptions rather than give an exact-looking answer to an incomplete hand.

Future episode pages can place results and later commentary in a closed, clearly labeled spoiler section. Chapter titles and the rest of an account can still reveal parts of a story, so watch the video first when the outcome matters to you.
