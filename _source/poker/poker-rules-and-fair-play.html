Clarify facts without announcing an unintended action.

Before acting, ask for the amount or the action to be clarified. TDA Rule 3 warns that action terms used in questions can be interpreted as a declaration. Neutral questions reduce avoidable ambiguity. For a dispute, ask for the floor and describe the sequence, amounts and statements without deciding the penalty yourself.

Preserve the distinction between a rules question and strategic advice. A dealer can clarify an amount; another person should not choose your action. Keep filming, note-taking and devices within the event's actual restrictions.

A minimum raise follows the increment, not a doubling rule.

Fictional no-limit sequence

A bets 100 and B raises to 250, an increment of 150. The next full raise is to 400. If C instead goes all-in for 340, the 90 increase does not reopen B’s raising option in this sequence.

The example illustrates the full-raise and reopening distinction in TDA Rules 45 and 49. Players who have not acted, cumulative short all-ins and limit games require their own checks. Read the applicable examples or ask the floor instead of extending this one sequence into a universal shortcut.

“Between hands” does not necessarily mean “allowed.”

PokerStars' third-party-tools policy distinguishes tools permitted generally, tools prohibited always, and tools restricted while its software is open. That last category is not limited to the seconds when a player faces a decision. A tool's marketing claim that it is compliant is not the operator's approval.

GGPoker's Security & Ecology Policy is a separate operator document with its own treatment of external assistance, software and player conduct. Do not transfer a permission from one room to another, or assume that a second device changes the rule.

For this site, the intended use is before or after a playing session, away from the table. Close the playing client when its policy requires that separation. These links are policy references, not endorsements, location-eligibility checks or permission to use this site's search during play.

Report a concern with a record, not a public accusation.

Keep the event or table identifier, time, relevant hand reference and a factual description. Separate what you directly observed from what you inferred. Send concerns through the room's appropriate private channel rather than posting another player's identifying information, account data or unsupported accusations.

Ask what information is needed and preserve your own relevant records without covert collection. Do not buy mined opponent histories or try to investigate by breaking the operator's rules yourself. For account-security problems, use the operator's authenticated support route, not a link from an unsolicited message.

Make practical needs part of preparation.

Ask the organizer ahead of time about seating, communication or other accommodations you need. TDA Rule 10 addresses accommodations where possible; it is not a guarantee that a particular event has the arrangements you require. Confirm the actual plan before traveling.

For gambling-related distress or difficulty controlling play, the support directory offers a separate route. Help is not something to postpone until you have studied more or recovered a loss.

Check the scope.

A site allows a tool. Does that authorize using it in every poker room?

No. Policies apply to the operator and circumstances they describe. Check the current policy for the exact game and platform.

After a bet of 100 and a raise to 250, why is the next full raise to 400?

The last full raise increment was 150. Adding that to 250 gives 400. This fictional no-limit example assumes no short-stack or reopening complication.

Does a suspicious-looking fold prove collusion?

No. Record the observable facts and report a concern privately. A single action and an interpretation are not proof of another person's intent.
