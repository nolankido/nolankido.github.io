What later analysis can establish

I was called by a strong hand and caught a four. The favorable result does not prove the shove was good, just as losing would not prove it was bad. At a reported 9.87 BB, blind pressure matters, but so do the big blind's effective stack, calling frequencies, payout structure, and risk of elimination. My statement that I would make this shove again is a personal judgment, not a verified solver result or a universal any-two-card recommendation.

Alternatives described in the narration

I describe the button as aggressive and consider calling, making a smaller raise or moving all-in. I say I disliked calling out of position because overcards often make later streets difficult for a medium pair. That account was narrated afterward; no contemporaneous written decision record is available.

Review without knowing the winner

The 75,000 open is 2.5 BB, and my stated 585,000 stack is approximately 19.5 BB. A reshove can succeed when the button folds and can retain showdown equity when called. Which action has the greatest tournament value depends on the button's actual opening and calling ranges, other players' stacks, the short small-blind stack and the payout ladder. Holding against A-T offsuit demonstrates the actual outcome, not that my shove was theoretically best.
