Free after-play practice / Fixed fictional examples

Pot odds sensitivity lab

Change a hypothetical equity or fold-rate input and watch the conditional chip value move. The pot sizes are fixed from the original Pot Odds Workshop, so you can focus on one assumption at a time. Every calculation happens in this browser, with no external services or account.

Scope: These three scenarios are fictional, heads-up, matched-pot chip models. They omit rake, future betting, side pots, bounties and payout-sensitive ICM. Equity and fold rates are assumptions, not opponent estimates or solved ranges. This tool is for study away from play, not at-table assistance.

Privacy: No real hands are entered. No forms, analytics, storage, cookies, remote scripts or requests are used by this page. The worked models remain readable without JavaScript.

1. River call: an uncertain equity band

A 20 BB pot faces a 10 BB bet. A call ends action with no deductions. Equity includes shares of tied pots.

Pot when facing action
30 BB
Additional call
10 BB
Final matched pot
40 BB
Break-even equity
25%

Move in 1 percentage point steps.

22% to 29%: modeled call EV -1.20 to +1.60 BB. The band crosses break-even.

Each equity point changes call EV by 0.40 BB. Lower and upper bounds may be reversed; the calculation sorts them.

Show the fixed call formula

Current pot P = 20 + 10 = 30 BB. Additional call C = 10 BB. Final pot F = P + C = 40 BB. Call EV against folding is (assumed equity / 100) × 40 - 10 BB. The break-even equity is 10 / 40 = 25%. This is not a recommendation to call without an evidenced range.

2. Facing a raise to 14 BB

Start with 10 BB. You bet 4 BB. An opponent raises to 14 BB total on the street. Assume a call ends action.

Current pot
28 BB
Additional call
10 BB, not 14 BB
Final matched pot
38 BB
Break-even equity
5/19 ≈ 26.32%

Not a known or observed opponent range.

25%: modeled call EV -0.50 BB, below break-even.

Show the raise-to ledger

Current pot = 10 + 4 + 14 = 28 BB. Additional call = 14 - 4 = 10 BB. Final pot = 38 BB. Call EV is (assumed equity / 100) × 38 - 10 BB. The old 4 BB already committed is not paid again.

3. Pure bluff: the bettor and caller have different prices

A zero-showdown-equity bettor risks 6 BB into a 10 BB pot. The opponent folds or calls, never raises. Checking is assigned zero chip EV.

Bet size
6 BB
Pot before bet
10 BB
Bettor's break-even folds
37.5%
Caller's break-even equity
3/11 ≈ 27.27%

This does not estimate how frequently any real opponent folds.

40% folds: modeled pure-bluff EV +0.40 BB, above break-even.

Show the separate actor prices

The bettor breaks even with folds at 6 / (10 + 6) = 37.5%. Bluff EV is (fold probability / 100) × 10 - (1 - fold probability / 100) × 6 BB. The caller is offered 6 to call into a final pot of 10 + 6 + 6 = 22 BB, requiring 6 / 22 = 3/11 or about 27.27% showdown equity. These thresholds belong to different players.

Before drawing any conclusion

  • Write the exact information known at the decision and identify which range weights were invented.
  • Check whether another action, player or street changes the eligible pot or objective.
  • Try at least two plausible assumptions. If modeled EV changes sign, report the sensitivity instead of declaring an action solved.
  • Do not use this tool during live play when the event restricts assistance.
