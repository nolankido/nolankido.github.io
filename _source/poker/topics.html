Scope / Trust

A map, not a claim to cover everything.

The directory currently emphasizes hold'em explanations, tournament preparation, study tools and original research. Cash games and mixed games have starting guides and outside sources, not complete in-house courses. Room information, home-game setup, poker history and media mostly point to other publishers.

This site does not maintain live seat availability, a complete worldwide room database, current tournament results, or country-by-country gambling and tax advice. Follow the relevant source and check the date. A listing review date describes the work recorded on that listing; reorganizing the directory does not mean every source was re-reviewed today.

Selected links are editorial starting points, not paid placements, performance rankings or a promise that a product suits your game. Free access describes the linked resource, not every product sold by its publisher. Read the review and access-label policy before comparing resources.

Use the guided reading collections for longer study paths, the guide library for local explanations, and the external directory for detailed format and experience filters. Suggest a missing topic or report an error without sending private hand histories or other players' identifying details.
