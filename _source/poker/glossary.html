Seats and stack sizes

BB / big blind
A forced live wager; also the unit used to compare stack sizes. BB can label a seat.
SB / small blind
The forced wager immediately before the big blind in the seating order.
BTN / button
The marker defining nominal dealer position, not a guarantee of last action preflop.
UTG
The first preflop acting position after the blinds at a table with more than two players.
CO / cutoff
The seat immediately before the button.
HJ / hijack
The seat two places before the button.
Effective stack
For two players, the smaller relevant stack, limiting what they can contest against each other.
IP / OOP
In position means acting after an opponent on a street; out of position means acting before.

Betting language

Check
Pass without adding chips when there is nothing to call.
Call
Match the outstanding wager, or call all-in for the amount available.
Fold
Give up the hand and any claim to its pots.
Raise to / by
“To” gives the new total wager; “by” gives the increment over the previous wager.
Three-bet / 3-bet
A reraise. Preflop the big blind counts as bet one. Not a three-times sizing rule.
Jam / shove
Informal terms for betting or raising all-in.
Limp
Call the big blind preflop instead of opening with a raise.
Check-raise
Check, then raise a bet when the action returns.

Cards and boards

Hole cards
The two private cards dealt to a hold'em player.
Board
The shared community cards.
Street
A betting stage: preflop, flop, turn, or river.
Suited / offsuit
Hole cards with matching suits / different suits. AKs means suited; AKo means offsuit.
Kicker
An unpaired side card used in comparing the best five-card hands.
Set / trips
Both are three of a kind; a set uses a pocket pair, trips uses a board pair and one matching hole card.
Overpair / top pair
A pocket pair above every board rank / a pair matching the highest board rank.
Rainbow / two-tone / monotone flop
Three different suits / two cards of one suit / all three cards of one suit.

Draws and analysis

Draw
A holding needing further cards to complete a combination such as a straight or flush.
Gutshot / open-ended
A straight draw missing an internal rank / one with either end available. Winning outs still need checking.
Range
Possible holdings assigned to a player, often with weights. It is a model, not hidden-card knowledge.
Equity
The expected share of a pot under specified hands or ranges and future cards, including ties.
Pot odds
The price of a call relative to the pot; a simple break-even percentage uses call ÷ final pot.
Blocker
A known card that removes some otherwise possible opponent holdings.
C-bet / continuation bet
Commonly a flop bet by the preflop raiser. The label does not establish hand strength.
Value bet / bluff
A wager seeking calls from worse hands / seeking folds from better hands; some bets serve multiple purposes.

Tournament context

Ante / BBA
A forced pot contribution separate from live blinds; BBA means big-blind ante.
Bubble / ITM
The boundary before a payout threshold / in the money, qualifying for a payout.
Pay jump / min-cash
The increase between payout levels / the smallest scheduled cash award.
Re-entry / bullet
Another permitted paid entry / informal language for an entry or attempt.
Freezeout
An event allowing no re-entry or rebuy.
Satellite
A tournament awarding entry or tickets to another event under its prize structure.
Bounty
An elimination-related prize; eligibility and award mechanics depend on the format.
Bagging
Recording and securing a stack for a later stage; not proof of a payout.

Reading the story

Hero / villain
Analysis labels for the focal player and opponent. “Villain” is not a character judgment.
Showdown
Comparing remaining eligible hands after betting ends.
Main pot / side pot
Separate pools contested by players with different matched contributions.
Runout
The remaining community cards as they are dealt.
Double-up
Informal description of approximately doubling a stack, often through a called all-in.
Cooler / bad beat
Informal labels for strong-hand collisions / losing after being favored. Neither proves correct play.
Tank
Take time over a decision. An edit does not establish how long the actual decision took.
Reconstructed hand
An account rebuilt from notes, memory, or footage; estimates should remain identified.
