About this library

A reading time is an estimate, not a deadline.

Estimates use the article source text, including answer explanations, at an assumed 225 words per minute, rounded up. They exclude the shared navigation and related-guide cards. Practice, calculation and taking notes can take longer. This is an editorial convention, not a measurement of your reading speed.

The guide labels describe a suggested starting point, not a qualification. Worked hands and monetary examples are explicitly fictional. They explain a model or a decision without claiming to be a verified solution to a hand you are playing.

Search and topic filters only narrow the guide cards already on this page. The site does not send or save search terms, put them in the address bar, or track filter choices. If the filtering script is blocked, every guide remains available below the introduction. Ordinary pageview analytics is covered in the privacy notice.
