About this poker project

The story and the study belong together.

The aim is a readable account of tournament poker, with enough detail to inspect a decision afterward. The guides support that account; they are not homework before a story.

From the table

Actual accounts and reconstructed hands distinguish recorded action from recollection. Important unknowns stay visible.

Decision Lab

Invented teaching cases let you pause and test an explanation. They are labeled as fiction, not personal results.

After the session

Later review can change an explanation without rewriting what was knowable at the table. A behind-the-edit account can show how an explanation is made.
