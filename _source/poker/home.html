03

How to read this

The record and the review are not the same thing.

The account

Actual footage, recorded action, and remembered details have different limits. Reconstructed hands should make those limits visible.

The decision

Thinking at the table belongs beside the information available then, not a result learned afterward.

The review

Later analysis is an interpretation. An illustrative hand is labeled as an example, not presented as a personal result.

Keep in touch

A hand, a question, a different perspective.

For a correction or a question, include the page title and the point you are referring to. There is no need to share another player's name or private information.
