How this directory grows

Broad coverage. A reason for every link.

Free describes the listed reading or resource. Mixed means free and paid material coexist. Paid identifies a commercial product or viewing library, even when its description is free. Features, registration, and regional access can change.

Each listing records its publisher, purpose, access type, suggested audience, and review date. Page read means the destination's public text or catalogue was inspected. It does not mean every article, paid course, download, calculation, or video was tested. Search index only means the official listing was confirmed but direct page reading was unavailable; that limitation is kept visible.

Discovery begins with original publishers, official rules and schedules, and references from useful articles. The next step is checking the destination, writing a specific description, removing duplicates and tracking parameters, and deciding where it belongs. Search results do not automatically become recommendations. New links are released in reviewed batches.

Some linked sites carry advertising, affiliate offers, gambling promotions, or paid upgrades. The links added here contain no affiliate or referral parameters. A listing is not a commercial partnership, a product endorsement, or permission to use a tool during play. Use study software after the session and follow the actual venue or operator's rules.

A large count is not evidence of being the best directory. Useful coverage, source diversity, clear access, working reader paths and honest review limits matter more. Schedules and products receive earlier review priority than stable explanations. A successful network response alone does not establish that the content is still relevant. A changed domain, new paywall, or broken destination requires review. No continuous link monitoring or automatic publication is implied by a review date.

Prepared with AI assistance. Descriptions are original summaries of public source pages. Reading and filtering here adds no custom analytics events or saved searches; the existing pageview privacy notice still applies. External sites use their own privacy and access policies when you open them.
