What I pay attention to in a poker hand.

The most useful hand record starts with what I actually knew at the table: position, blinds, effective stacks, action, bet sizes and the board. Missing numbers and uncertain recollections should be labeled rather than reconstructed as if they were certain.

After a tournament, I can review the alternatives, study the price of a call, or consider tournament pressure. That later analysis is not what I necessarily thought during play. A fortunate result does not prove the decision was sound, and an unfavorable result does not prove it was wrong.

For the method, read Review a hand, not just the result and the example of a hand explained before and after an edit. The worked hands there are marked as instructional examples, not mislabeled as my personal tournament history.
