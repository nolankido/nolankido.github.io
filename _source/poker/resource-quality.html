What do the review labels mean?

Public page read means the public content or catalogue page was inspected within the limitations on that listing. It does not mean a paid book was read, an entire podcast heard, software accuracy tested, or every linked page verified.

Search index only means the direct destination was not adequately accessible for a page review. The listing carries that limitation explicitly. Prefer a directly inspectable official source for an important current decision.

Free, Paid and Mixed describe the resource being listed. Free documentation can explain a paid product; a free review is not free access to the book. Read the description and caution together.
