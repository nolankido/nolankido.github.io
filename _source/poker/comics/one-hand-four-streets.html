A few details to keep straight

The four streets are betting rounds. Showdown is the comparison of hands, not a fifth betting round. A round can check through without anyone making a new bet. If players are all-in and no further betting is possible, the remaining board cards are dealt without more betting.

The AI-drawn card faces are not a verified deal. In the showdown panel, K-Q can make a pair of kings with a king on the board, but that alone does not establish a winner. Compare every remaining player’s best five-card hand; equal best hands share the pot.

When everyone else folds, the remaining player wins without a showdown and normally does not need to show their cards. Follow the room’s rules about exposing cards.

The comic in words

This accessible companion follows the six panels and explains the sequence. Use the written notes above when the artwork simplifies a rule.

  1. Preflop: your two hole cards

    A new player asks whether another card is coming. The dealer explains that everyone receives two face-down hole cards, then the first betting round takes place. The community-card board is still empty.

  2. The flop: three community cards

    Three face-up cards arrive together. The player wants to see another immediately, but the dealer reminds them that the table must finish this betting round before moving on.

  3. The turn: one more card

    The fourth community card joins the board. The player notices that only one card arrived this time. Another betting round follows.

  4. The river: the final card

    The fifth community card completes the board. The dealer announces the final betting round.

  5. Showdown: compare the hands

    The player says K-Q makes a pair of kings with the king on the board. Another character calls it a winner. That is the comic’s intended ending, but the drawing does not establish the result: a pair wins only if no eligible opponent has a better hand.

  6. A hand can end early

    Two players announce that they are folding. The last player remaining wins the pot, even if the hand has not reached the river. You can win by having the best hand at showdown or by being the only player who has not folded.

Rules reference: PokerStars’ Texas Hold’em rules. To practice the sequence, follow one complete hand from deal to showdown.
