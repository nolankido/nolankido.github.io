Count five cards, every time

At showdown, choose your strongest five-card combination from your two hole cards and the five community cards. Hold’em allows zero, one or two hole cards. Equal best five-card hands split the pot; neither a sixth card nor a suit breaks the tie.

The small AI-drawn card faces can be inconsistent. The complete deals below are separate, illustrative examples of the comic’s lessons. Playing the board does not guarantee a split: an opponent may improve on it.

The comic in words

  1. The showdown

    A player expects an ace in their hand to settle the result. The dealer slows them down: first identify the best five cards available to each player still in the hand.

  2. How it works

    Your private cards do not have to appear in your final hand. Select whichever five-card combination is strongest, whether it uses both hole cards, just one, or the entire board.

  3. Example 1: two hole cards

    You hold A♥ K♥. The board is A♣ K♠ 9♣ 5♦ 2♠. Your best hand is A♥ A♣ K♥ K♠ 9♣: two pair, aces and kings, with a nine kicker. Both hole cards contribute.

  4. Example 2: one hole card

    You hold J♠ 3♣. The board is A♣ K♦ Q♥ 10♠ 2♦. Your best hand is A♣ K♦ Q♥ J♠ 10♠: an ace-high straight. Only your jack contributes; the three is outside your best five.

  5. Example 3: zero hole cards

    You hold 2♦ 3♣. The board is 8♣ 7♦ 6♥ 5♠ 4♣. Your best hand is the board’s eight-high straight. Neither hole card improves it. An opponent holding a nine could make a nine-high straight and beat you.

  6. The tiebreaker myth

    On that same 8♣ 7♦ 6♥ 5♠ 4♣ board, A♥ K♥ and Q♠ J♦ both play the eight-high straight. The ace does not help because it is outside the best five. If these are the only two players contesting the pot, they split it.

Does the kicker count?

A kicker matters when it belongs to the best five-card hand. With a pair, compare the pair first, then the three side cards in order. With a straight, all five places are already occupied: compare the highest card in each straight. There is no extra kicker.
