These worksheets accompany the notes. Each includes a short guide, a completed fictional example, and a blank editable copy. Read the example first, then use only the parts that help with your situation.

These three worksheets need no account. Download a copy and fill it in privately in a text editor or notes app. This website does not collect your answers. Keep completed copies, especially those containing personal information, out of public repositories.
