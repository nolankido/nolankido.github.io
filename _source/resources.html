Use what helps

A place to begin, not another system to maintain.

These worksheets accompany the notes. Each includes a short guide, a completed fictional example, and a blank editable copy. Read the example first, then use only the parts that help with your situation.

Nothing here needs an account. Download a copy and fill it in privately in a text editor or notes app. This website does not collect your answers. Keep completed copies, especially those containing personal information, out of public repositories.

The templates are thinking aids, not validated assessments or substitutes for qualified advice. A conversation, consent, or rest may be more useful than completing another form.
