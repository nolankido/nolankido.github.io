Visiting the website

This website uses Umami Cloud for pageview analytics. It does not add advertising pixels. There are no accounts, public comments, or embedded social feeds.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure their services. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Website analytics

Umami Cloud helps me understand which pages people read and how they find this site. Its reports include pageviews, referring websites, browser and device information, and approximate location such as country. Umami describes its tracking code as cookie-free and does not track visitors across websites. See the Umami documentation and Umami privacy policy.

This installation records pageviews only. It does not send contact-form fields, names, email addresses, or message contents as analytics events. URL query strings and fragments are excluded from recorded page URLs, and the tracker is configured to respect your browser's Do Not Track setting. There are no session recordings or advertising profiles added by this site.

Your browser contacts Umami to load the script and send analytics requests, which necessarily involves technical network information such as your IP address. The website and contact form remain usable when analytics is blocked. Hosting and form providers have separate practices described on this page.

Optional Poker Foundations progress

The Poker Study desk lets you mark six lessons and choose a next reading or practice task. Those choices remain in the open page unless you choose to remember them on this device. That option uses one entry in your browser's local storage. It contains the lesson marks you choose, your next task, and the course and file-format versions. It contains no names, written answers, hand histories, scores, or record of the pages you visit.

The progress tool does not send these choices to this website, analytics, or another service. You can stop remembering them or reset the record from the same controls. Other people using the same browser profile on this device may be able to see it. Browser clearing, private browsing, or storage limits may remove it, so keep an exported copy if the record matters to you.

Exporting downloads a small progress file. Importing a file reads it in the current page and shows a preview before replacing your choices; it does not upload the file or enable device saving. Manual copy and paste use the complete progress text shown in the tool. Your browser and any app you use to keep or share a copy have their own data handling. Ordinary pageview analytics still operates as described above, without progress events.

Typography

The site uses Schibsted Grotesk, Source Serif 4, and IBM Plex Mono, originally obtained from Google Fonts under open licenses. Font declarations and font files are served by nolankido.com through the existing GitHub Pages hosting. Loading these typefaces does not require a request to Google. The site remains readable with local fallback fonts when font files cannot load.

Using the contact form

The form requires your name, email address, and message. Topic and additional context are optional. This information is used to receive, organize, and respond to correspondence.

Formspark receives form submissions and provides delivery and spam-filtering services. Cloudflare Turnstile is loaded on the contact page to help prevent automated abuse; it may process technical browser and network information for verification. These services have their own privacy terms: Formspark privacy policy and Cloudflare privacy policy.

Messages may be processed by email services and AI-assisted tools used to organize correspondence. Do not send passwords, financial account details, medical records, identity documents, or other confidential material through this form.

This site does not save unfinished form messages in browser storage. If a submission fails, its contents remain in the open form so you can copy them or retry. Reloading or closing the page may discard them.

Keeping and sharing a page

Copy-link controls copy only a page's permanent address after you press the button. On supported devices, Share page opens your browser's sharing interface with the public title and permanent address, not calculator entries or worksheet answers. Your clipboard, browser, and any app you choose have their own data handling. These controls add no sharing analytics events.

Links and published notes
