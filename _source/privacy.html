Visiting the website

This website does not add advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, or a visitor-tracking dashboard. There are no accounts, public comments, or embedded social feeds.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure their services. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Typography

The site requests Schibsted Grotesk, Source Serif 4, and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to retrieve the stylesheet and font files, which can disclose technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. The site remains readable with local fallback fonts when those requests are blocked. See the Google Fonts privacy information.

Using the contact form

The form requires your name, email address, and message. Topic and additional context are optional. This information is used to receive, organize, and respond to correspondence.

Formspark receives form submissions and provides delivery and spam-filtering services. Cloudflare Turnstile is loaded on the contact page to help prevent automated abuse; it may process technical browser and network information for verification. These services have their own privacy terms: Formspark privacy policy and Cloudflare privacy policy.

Messages may be processed by email services and AI-assisted tools used to organize correspondence. Do not send passwords, financial account details, medical records, identity documents, or other confidential material through this form.

This site does not save unfinished form messages in browser storage. If a submission fails, its contents remain in the open form so you can copy them or retry. Reloading or closing the page may discard them.

Links and published notes

External references open only when you choose to follow them. Other websites have their own privacy practices. Notes identify AI assistance where it was used and distinguish illustrative examples from research findings.

Privacy questions and corrections

For a question about this site, a correction, or a request concerning information you submitted, use the contact form and choose “Privacy question or correction.” Include only the details needed to identify the request.
