Books / Author catalog

Which books does Nolan Kido describe here?

My two nonfiction book projects cover different practical questions. One Workflow at a Time is about testing whether an AI-assisted job is reliable and worthwhile in a small business. How to Play No-Limit Texas Hold’em explains the rules, prices, ranges and reasoning behind poker decisions.

Each title has its own canonical author page, a bounded description, and original free related exercises. You can read the free methods without buying a book, and the descriptions here do not imply that every chapter or edition has been independently inspected.

Start / Free exercises

Can I try these ideas without purchasing a book?

Yes. The site's related exercises are freely readable and do not require registration, a paid AI account, or a poker wager. They are original companion materials, not copies of book chapters.

Practical AI: In an invented comparison, a manual update takes 12 minutes and an AI-assisted version takes 15 minutes after preparation and checking. Read the whole-task example and decide what would justify another test.

Poker: In a fictional river example, a starting pot of 20 counters, a bet of 10 and a call of 10 yield a 40-counter final pot. The simplified chip-equity threshold is 25%, but range and game assumptions still matter. Read the pot-odds example.
