# Viewer library: understand the decisions

Adds six original beginner follow-on guides: position-and-stacks, reading-the-board,
ranges-and-bets, tournament-formats, table-etiquette and follow-along. The library
now has thirteen topic guides, three reading routes and eight phrase shortcuts.
The new pages contain 24 native answer disclosures, including nine in the three
follow-along scenes. A watching checklist is available as editable Markdown.

The examples do not describe real videos or results. No channel, photograph,
private hand, player identity or real tournament story is invented. The original
48-entry glossary and seven topic guides retain their URLs and source contents.
The Technology-first homepage, non-Poker page bytes, contact behavior, original
stylesheets, episode catalog and general Notes feed remain unchanged.

References were checked October 5, 2026. TDA guidance is the September 7, 2026
version 1.0; local event rules and organizer-specific deviations still matter.
Definitions are not a strategy chart. Explicit assumptions accompany every
betting or range calculation. Viewing does not require wagering.
