## Fictional river question

Two players remain with no rake, fees, side pots, bounty, future
betting or tournament payout effects in this simplified chip model.
The pot contains 20 counters before your opponent bets 10.

Starting pot: 20
Opponent river bet: 10
Pot including that bet: ______
Extra amount you must call: ______
Final pot after your call: ______
Break-even chip-equity fraction: ______

Explain why the denominator includes the chips you would call.

## Explain what remains uncertain

Is the threshold a recommendation to call? _______________

## Answer under these assumptions

After the bet the pot is **20 + 10 = 30 counters**.
Your additional call costs **10 counters**.
The pot after calling is **30 + 10 = 40 counters**.
The simplified break-even chip equity is **10 / 40 = 25%**.

This is not an instruction to call. Your actual showdown equity
depends on the opponent's possible holdings and their frequencies.
Fees, side pots, payouts and other game conditions may change the
relevant math. A correct pot fraction is not a complete strategy.
