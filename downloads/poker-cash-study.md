# Cash-game preparation and study record

Blank private-use sheet. Use before or after play, not as live assistance.
A fictional hand is enough. No gambling session or paid software is required.
Keep this copy yourself; do not submit personal answers to the website.

## Five study outputs
1. Game description: positions, relevant stacks, action and unknowns.
2. Pot ledger: current pot, extra call, final award and cost assumptions.
3. Two hypothetical ranges: combinations, blocked cards and changed assumptions.
4. Multiway sketch: who can still act and who is eligible for each pot.
5. Conditional conclusion: facts, assumptions, uncertainty and one next question.
