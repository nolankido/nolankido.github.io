# Tournament entry and prize-pool reconciliation sheet

Copy into a private note. This blank sheet is for checking an event's published terms and your own actual spending. It is not a live-event quote, a profit forecast or a verified statement of any organizer's receipts.

## 3. Separate people from paid entries

- Unique first entrants:
- Number of permitted and actually paid re-entries:
- **Total paid entries** (initial entries + paid re-entries):
- Other entries with special treatment, refunds or complimentary status:
- Sources and uncertainty in the counts:

For the simple equal-price case:

- Collected entry payments = full entry price × paid entries.
- Ordinary contributions = ordinary contribution per entry × eligible paid entries.
- Initial bounty funding = bounty amount per entry × eligible paid entries.
- Fees = fee per entry × eligible paid entries.
- Check: collected payments equal all component totals before any separate sponsor or organizer funding.

If entries have different prices or refunds, use separate rows for each group and sum only the eligible paid amounts.

## 4. Guarantees and ordinary payout ladder

- Exact guarantee wording and source:
- Is it ordinary prizes only, bounties only, combined, or something else?
- Are deductions allowed before or after the guarantee?
- Eligible contributed amount from paid entries:

- Final ordinary payout total:
- Sum of each actual ordinary finishing-place payment:

Do not count a bounty allocation twice, reuse a base payout schedule after adding an overlay, or infer a guarantee's scope from the headline alone.
