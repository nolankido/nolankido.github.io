# Poker episode planning sheet

Blank private-use worksheet. This is a proposed workflow, not a claim that an
episode exists. Keep filenames, raw notes and completed sheets private.

## Graphics and sound review

- Board is revealed in the right order:
- Opponent cards are not shown as though known earlier:
- Pot and bet sizes agree with the record:
- Estimates are visibly approximate:
- Any equity number has a defensible calculation and information set:
- Cards, action and stakes are readable on a small screen:
- Captions correctly identify ranks, suits and poker terms:
- Later reactions are not presented as contemporaneous:
