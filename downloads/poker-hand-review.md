# Poker hand review

A private, editable record for study after play. A thinking aid, not a solved strategy recommendation.
Keep other people's identifying information out of any copy you share.

## 4. What you thought at the table
Preserve the earliest available thinking record before writing a later review. If none exists, label the account as a later recollection, not a contemporaneous at-table note. Do not rewrite it afterward.
