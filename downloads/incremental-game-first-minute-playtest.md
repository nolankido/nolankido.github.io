## Start a 60-second run

1. Press **Start over** in the demo.
2. Start a separate stopwatch and ask the player to try the game without coaching.
3. Observe what they do and what they say. Do not interpret their motive yet.
4. At 60 seconds, note the counters and end the timed observation.

## Interpretation boundaries

- A single run is not a finding about all players or a retention study.
- Sparks produced are not a measurement of enjoyment or design quality.
- A visible browser tab and an active passive-production timer are needed for a comparable run.
- Distinguish the player's exact words from the observer's explanation.
- Obtain consent before observing someone else or sharing their words.
- Keep this completed worksheet private unless sharing was explicitly agreed.
- No upload, account, AI subscription, or analytics service is required.
