# Tool trust check

A thinking aid for one limited claim, not a certification or proof that a tool is safe for every use. Write down the expectation before looking at the tool's answer.

## Limits

A passing example does not establish correctness for all inputs. A correct calculation does not establish that the data are representative or the interpretation is appropriate. Higher-consequence uses need proportionate scrutiny and relevant expertise. Do not relabel an estimate as a guarantee.
