AI ANSWER QUICK CHECK                         Nolan Kido
https://nolankido.com/technology/check-ai-answers/

Use this one-claim checklist before relying on an AI answer.
Keep it in your own notes. This file collects or sends nothing.

1. NAME THE ACTION
   What would I do, send, publish, decide, or run if I believed it?
2. ISOLATE ONE CHECKABLE CLAIM
   Copy the exact sentence, number, promise, or code behavior.
   Keep the source input or original instruction beside it.
3. CHOOSE INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE
   Fact: open an original publisher or primary record and passage.
   Number: repeat the arithmetic from inputs, including all costs.
   Summary: compare dates, conditions, owners, and certainty.
   Message: check commitments against what was authorized.
   Code: test a case with an independently expected outcome.
   Advice: verify facts and ask whose priorities are assumed.
   Two agreeing chatbots are not independent confirmation.
4. RECORD WHAT THE CHECK ACTUALLY SHOWED
   Note the evidence source, date or version, and exact result.
   Make a test capable of failing on a plausible wrong answer.
5. CHOOSE ONE STATUS FOR THIS CLAIM
   SUPPORTED: the evidence fits this exact claim and conditions.
   CONTRADICTED: the evidence disagrees with the claim.
   UNRESOLVED: evidence is missing, limited, or inconclusive.
   Silence is not proof that an event did not happen.
6. DECIDE WHAT TO DO NEXT
   Use only the checked part for the stated purpose, revise it,
   seek better evidence, or leave the claim unused. Keep the
   decision with the responsible person. Significant legal,
   medical, financial, or safety actions need qualified review.

Example: A fictional AI summary says a Sunday meeting is
confirmed; the source says it may happen if weather allows.
The summary misstates the source. Revise to "A Sunday meeting
is possible, weather permitting." The event status is still
unverified. This exercise is not a recorded model output.
