## Fictional example and answer

A fictional small-business weekly update takes **12 minutes** manually.
The AI-assisted attempt requires 4 minutes preparing sources, 3 drafting,
7 checking the facts and 1 correcting an error.
The assisted total is **4 + 3 + 7 + 1 = 15 minutes**.
Difference: 12 - 15 = **-3 minutes**. The method takes three minutes longer.

These numbers were invented to illustrate a decision. They are
not measured savings or a testimonial. A fast draft alone does
not establish a useful workflow.
