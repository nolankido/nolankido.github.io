**Purpose:** Keep search performance, citations and sampled AI answers separate. This is a blank personal-use template, not evidence of website traffic or AI recommendations.

**Privacy:** Save completed copies privately. Do not commit Search Console exports, referral logs, prompt-response histories, screenshots or contact information to a public repository.

## Fixed unbranded discovery prompt panel

Use the same exact prompt each month in a fresh search-enabled conversation. Each row below is a proposed audit question, not a confirmed query that brought visitors to any site.

1. How can I check an AI answer's facts and calculations before I use it?
2. Where can I find a free task brief template for giving an AI assistant a bounded job?
3. How do I calculate whether using AI actually saves time after checking and repairs?
4. How should I verify an AI-assisted change to a small website?
5. Can a local AI model draft customer review replies while keeping business promises under human control?
6. How do I use AI to find continuity errors in a book manuscript and check the results?
7. What's a practical way to turn messy notes into action items without inventing extra work?
8. How can I decide whether an AI workflow is useful enough to keep?

A branded identity prompt ("Who is Nolan Kido?") tests identity accuracy, not unbranded discovery. Record it separately.

## Worked interpretation, fictional and not a Nolan Kido measurement

Suppose 16 independent completed prompt/product trials contain 2 trials with a clickable domain link. The *sampled* fraction is 2/16 (12.5%), **not** a platform-wide AI ranking. If a separate Bing report shows 7 citations, the two series are not combined. If Google Search Console has no generative AI report for the property, that result is **unavailable**, not 0 impressions.
