# Poker vlog watching checklist

Use this privately while watching a recorded video or studying afterward.
Not for live-play assistance. No answers are collected by the website.

## Before the action

What game and event format are stated?
Who is the button? Which players are still in the hand?
What are the blinds and antes?
Are stacks measured before or after posting and betting?
What is the smaller relevant stack? Are there multiple side pots?

## At the decision

Which street is this: preflop, flop, turn or river?
What changed in the board's ranks, suits or pairs?
Is the pot displayed before or after the latest bet?
Is a raise stated as a total (to) or an increase (by)?
What additional amount would a call cost?
Would a call close the action, or can another player still raise?
