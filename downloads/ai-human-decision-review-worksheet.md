## The task and source record

A fictional community organizer wants a short public announcement for a free workshop. These are the only confirmed input facts, as recorded **Tuesday at 2:00 p.m.** in this invented scenario:

1. The workshop begins **Friday at 6:00 p.m.**
2. **Registration is required by Thursday at noon.** There is no approved walk-in policy.
3. The room holds **24 people**, and **18 registrations are confirmed** at the recorded time.
4. The organizer **has not confirmed accessibility arrangements**.
5. Only a designated organizer can **approve and publish** the announcement. An attendee list is private and is not an approved model input.

### Constructed draft to review

> Come Friday at 7:00 p.m.! All 24 spots are still open. Just walk in, and wheelchair accessibility is guaranteed. We can publish this automatically.

The quotation is written for this worksheet. It is **not** an actual model response.

**Count check:** 24 capacity minus 18 confirmed registrations = ______ unreserved places **at the stated snapshot**. Can the draft promise this number will remain available when someone reads it? ______.

**Human decision:** Mark one for the *current proposed public draft* and explain why.

- [ ] PROCEED IN SCOPE: continue private drafting only, with no public communication or commitment
- [ ] REVISE AND RECHECK: correct contradicted facts, then compare the full new text with the source
- [ ] HOLD PUBLICATION: missing authorization or a material claim still lacks verification
- [ ] STOP USING AI FOR THIS TASK: a necessary input or proposed action would violate privacy, permission, or other controlling rules

These can be stages of one workflow, not one magic verdict. For example, you may revise a private draft but still hold its publication.
