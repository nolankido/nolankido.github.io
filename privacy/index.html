Policy Privacy

Minimal collection by design.

This site is intentionally simple and avoids collecting information unless it is needed for a specific purpose.

Website

No advertising trackers. No behavioral analytics.

The public site does not intentionally use advertising pixels, cross-site tracking, or behavioral analytics. Standard hosting infrastructure may still create ordinary technical logs as part of serving web pages.

Contact form

Information is collected when you choose to send it.

The contact form asks for identifying and contextual information needed to evaluate and respond to a message. Submissions may be processed by service providers used to deliver, filter, and triage messages, including automated spam and relevance screening.

Please do not submit passwords, financial account information, medical records, identity documents, or other sensitive material through the public form.

Questions

For a privacy question related to this site or a contact-form submission, use the contact page and select “Other.”
