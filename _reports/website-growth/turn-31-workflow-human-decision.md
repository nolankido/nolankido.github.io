# Turn 31: turn the comparison into your decision

Prepared October 27, 2026, Pacific/Honolulu.

## Purpose

Complete the reader's final decision step in the existing AI Time Savings
calculator at `/technology/ai-time-savings/`. The operating map's F3 outcome and
the practical-AI Measure stage both call for a choice to keep, adjust, stop or
gather more observations. The blank worksheet already supports that choice.
The interactive export previously labeled its calculated suggestion `Decision`
without providing a place for the reader's own decision.

This turn follows published release
`962e489bb557ee6dfda3a48524a4bc8953f3c8e8`, tree
`c58a229171ec97246111faccc80d411969e42010`.

## Reader improvement

- Label the calculator's existing recommendation `Suggested next step`.
- Add an optional personal choice, reason and remaining uncertainty, and next
  review point. The review point can be a date, event or changed condition.
- Start with no decision recorded. The calculator never fills the choice from
  its arithmetic, a fictional example or the reader's quality assessment.
- Include the current personal record in the same text used for the visible
  report, copying, text download and printing. A comparison can still be saved
  while the decision remains unrecorded.
- Preserve the quality condition, estimate or observation basis, fictional
  example provenance, calculation, sensitivity explanation and limitations.
  A reader's choice does not override those conditions or verify the result.

## State and privacy boundaries

Changing a number, evidence basis or quality assessment invalidates the visible
comparison and export. It clears the personal choice while preserving the
reader's notes as drafts. After recalculating, the reader must choose again to
attach a decision to the changed comparison. Recalculating unchanged inputs
retains the choice. Editing the personal record updates all export surfaces
without recalculating the time model.

Confirmed clearing, confirmed fictional-example replacement and navigation
reset the record. Cancelling a clear or replacement preserves it. Notes are
plain text with the displayed length limits. Delayed clipboard completion
cannot announce success for a report that has since changed.

The page does not send or store entries. It reuses the existing browser-only
calculator and exports. The original blank worksheet and combined practical-AI
workbook remain the no-script route. No new page, download, account, submission,
analytics event or API is introduced.

## Acceptance and evidence limits

Focused checks exercise explicit decisions, retained draft notes and renewed
choices, unchanged recalculation, safe text handling, clearing and replacement,
stale clipboard status, export and print equality, and optional native controls.
Browser checks cover keyboard operation, narrow layouts, text spacing and the
unchanged no-script worksheet. Independent review, the complete unit suite,
all five browser groups and the aggregate gate are required before merge.

Final test receipts, Pages deployment and the complete published-response check
belong in the release PR. This preparation record alone does not establish that
those gates passed. Working controls do not establish a person's actual use,
productivity, satisfaction, search rankings or AI citations. The separate
firsthand case and voluntary visitor study remain open.
