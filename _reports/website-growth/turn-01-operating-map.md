## Six flagship briefs

### F1. Build a usable AI task brief

- **Canonical route:** `/technology/task-brief/`.
- **Reader and question:** Someone delegating a bounded task to AI: "How do I turn a vague request into a usable brief?"
- **Useful result:** A brief naming the purpose, permitted material, output, stopping point, boundaries and independent checks.
- **Existing asset:** A public page titled "AI Task Brief Builder & Prompt Template | Nolan Kido", also linked directly from the homepage's practical-work selection.
- **Next improvement:** In Turn 03, add three explicitly illustrative examples: meeting notes to a plan, a short article, and a small website change. Improve output readability and copy/download recovery without creating another generator.
- **One next action:** Choose a suitable check at `/technology/check-ai-answers/` before relying on the resulting work.
- **Evidence of utility:** Observe whether a new visitor can produce and retain a brief without assistance. Record where the task fails; a pageview or button click is not proof of a usable result.
- **Search hypothesis:** AI task brief template; write a clear AI task brief. Volumes and difficulty have not been established.

### F2. Check an AI answer

- **Canonical route:** `/technology/check-ai-answers/`.
- **Reader and question:** Someone who has an answer they may act on: "How do I check the facts, numbers, sources or code?"
- **Useful result:** One consequential claim independently checked, with the remaining uncertainty stated.
- **Existing asset:** A public guide titled "How to Check AI Answers: Facts, Math & Code | Nolan Kido"; the homepage already points readers toward it.
- **Next improvement:** In Turn 07, make the direct answer, worked example, evidence and limits easier to follow. Clearly label intentionally flawed exercise answers.
- **One next action:** Inspect the source-to-summary example at `/technology/check-ai-summary/` when practicing evidence checking.
- **Evidence of utility:** A reader can identify a specific error or limitation and explain the evidence supporting their conclusion. Another AI agreeing is not independent verification.
- **Search hypothesis:** How to check AI answers; verify AI citations. Keep the broad guide distinct from its summary-specific companion.

### F3. Decide whether an AI workflow is worth keeping

- **Canonical route:** `/technology/ai-time-savings/`.
- **Reader and question:** Someone evaluating a workflow: "Does this save effort after preparation, checking, repair and upkeep?"
- **Useful result:** A keep, adjust, stop or gather-more-evidence decision based on comparable-quality alternatives and explicit inputs.
- **Existing asset:** A public page titled "AI Time Savings Calculator: Count the Full Workflow | Nolan Kido", linked from the homepage's practical-work selection.
- **Next improvement:** In Turn 08, distinguish one-off from recurring work and explain which assumptions can reverse the result. Improve interpretation, not just arithmetic.
- **One next action:** Use `/living-well/did-the-tool-actually-help/` to consider the wider practical costs; finishing with a decision to stop is valid.
- **Evidence of utility:** A reader can state what was counted, which values are assumptions, and why the conclusion follows. Modeled savings are not Nolan's measured productivity.
- **Search hypothesis:** AI workflow time savings calculator; calculate AI review and repair time.

### F4. Leave with a manageable week

- **Canonical route:** `/living-well/weekly-reset/`.
- **Reader and question:** Someone feeling scattered: "How do I reset my week without creating another job?"
- **Useful result:** Identify what matters, release one unnecessary obligation and choose one manageable next action.
- **Existing asset:** A public guide titled "A weekly reset that does not become another job | Nolan Kido". The homepage already links to its worksheet family.
- **Next improvement:** In Turn 09, put the smallest useful version first, add a clearly illustrative completed example, and keep web and printable guidance consistent.
- **One next action:** Complete the existing worksheet at `/living-well/worksheets/#weekly-reset` privately.
- **Evidence of utility:** Observe whether a reader leaves with an actionable note and less confusion. No streak, life score, compulsory account, or health claim is needed.
- **Search hypothesis:** Simple weekly reset worksheet; weekly reset without overwhelm.

### F5. Prepare for a first live poker tournament

- **Canonical route:** `/poker/first-live-tournament/`.
- **Reader and question:** An adult beginner: "What should I prepare and what will happen when I arrive?"
- **Useful result:** A practical preparation sequence and a short list of venue-specific questions still requiring confirmation.
- **Existing asset:** A public guide titled "Your first live tournament, without the guesswork | Nolan Kido Poker".
- **Next improvement:** In Turn 12, organize arrival, registration, seating, action, breaks and leaving around one coherent checklist. Distinguish general information from changing venue policy.
- **One next action:** Plan the complete outing at `/poker/tournament-trip-budget/`.
- **Evidence of utility:** A beginner can explain the preparation steps and distinguish entry cost from total outing cost. Qualified instructional review is separate from software checks.
- **Search hypothesis:** First live poker tournament what to expect; first tournament preparation checklist.

### F6. Explain which poker hand wins

- **Canonical route:** `/poker/hand-rankings/`.
- **Reader and question:** An adult new player or viewer: "Which five cards count, and why does this hand win or tie?"
- **Useful result:** Correctly compare hands using best-five construction, kickers, two pair, full houses and shared boards.
- **Existing asset:** A public guide titled "Poker Hand Rankings: Which Hand Wins? | Nolan Kido". The Poker homepage already has a native quick-reference link to it, and the current sitemap contains the route.
- **Next improvement:** In Turn 14, improve the original visual reference and matching accessible/print explanations. Check physical cards and comparisons independently. Investigate actual discovery evidence before changing URLs or introducing duplicate ranking pages.
- **One next action:** Practice card construction at `/poker/free-poker-learning-path/#card-basics`.
- **Evidence of utility:** The learner can explain a changed comparison, not merely copy a ranking list. No profitability or validated-mastery claim follows from an exercise.
- **Search hypothesis:** Which poker hand wins; poker kickers and ties. These hypotheses do not establish search demand.

## Measurement contract

Maintain the numerical record privately. Every observation needs a source, date window, timezone, scope, definition, access status and limitations. Use `unknown` or null for unavailable measurements, not zero.

| Measure | Definition and source | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Google search clicks | Search Console's reported web-search clicks in a fixed, settled window | Not all-source visits or unique people |
| Property impressions | Search Console property-level appearances | Do not replace with summed page impressions |
| Page/section impressions | Returned page rows, grouped by the first path segment | A search can expose several site pages; section sums are not property totals |
| Named-brand searches | Within visible query rows, match Nolan Kido or nolankido, case-insensitively | Keep unreported queries unclassified; do not call the remainder non-brand |
| Visitor estimates | The existing Umami dashboard's own visitor metric, with its definition and coverage | Not a census; do not substitute Search Console clicks |
| Pageviews and landing pages | Preserve the dashboard's actual metric names; use landing-page counts only if available | Pageviews are not arrivals, task completions or visits |
| Referrals | Identifiable source domains and exact landing routes where available | Missing referrers do not establish direct or brand traffic; do not claim complete attribution |
| Utility | Voluntary observed attempts, assistance needed, actual outcome and feedback | Tests and downloads are not observed usefulness |
| Personal brand | Genuine references, accurate recognition, relevant inquiries and documented collaborations | No invented testimonials; no automatic inference from followers or rankings |
| AI visibility | Engine-provided reports or a documented sample of cited answers, kept distinct by system | Conversational query wording does not prove an AI citation; citations are not visits |

The implementation record `ANALYTICS.md` dates the Umami installation to October 6, 2026. Never treat the pre-installation part of an older reporting window as zero visitors. Before comparing sources, confirm dashboard timezone, observed ingestion and actual start coverage. Preserve the fixed 28-day Search Console baseline separately; an overlapping visitor comparison must state its own dates and coverage. A conservative first overlap excludes the possibly partial installation day.

Keep the four section prefixes separate: `/technology/`, `/living-well/`, `/poker/`, `/creative/`. Keep homepage, author, navigation and general Notes pages in an additional bucket rather than forcing them into Technology. Do not add unique visitors across sections to invent a property total.
