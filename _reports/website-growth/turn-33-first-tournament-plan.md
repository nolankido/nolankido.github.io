# Turn 33: a usable first-tournament plan

Prepared October 27, 2026, Pacific/Honolulu.

## Purpose

The operating map's F5 outcome is a practical preparation sequence and a short
list of venue-specific questions still requiring confirmation. The existing
first-live-tournament guide explained individual moments, but readers had to
assemble the sequence themselves and reach the end before finding the planner.
The planner's single unresolved-question line did not distinguish a source,
the answer's conditions, its status or the reader's resulting decision.

This turn follows published commit
`74e064631e59e7659826144390dc77d49d2022ed`, tree
`24495e6156dd0d5abf71c707fb7bbd0f195e4983`.

## Reader improvement

- Put a visible six-step plan near the beginning of
  `/poker/first-live-tournament/`, with an opening jump link.
- Connect preparation, registration, seating, unclear action, breaks or moves,
  and finishing to the existing detailed explanations through native links.
- Make the existing event planner available immediately as a download or a
  matching printable preview. Retain the separate trip-budget worksheet.
- Extend the existing planner with the question, answer and conditions,
  official source or organizer response, source date if stated, date checked,
  status and next action before paying.
- Keep confirmed, unresolved and conflicting information distinct. A blank
  field or absent warning does not confirm a detail. Conflicts remain questions
  for the organizer about the relevant event and flight.
- Add an explicit personal entry decision, including waiting for confirmation,
  watching if the venue permits it, or choosing another day.

This is original procedural planning guidance. It does not add a spending
recommendation, strategy advice, current venue-policy claim or personal
tournament account. Readers prepare before the outing and review after play;
the existing privacy and no-live-hand-assistance boundaries remain in place.

tournament-checklists page, `downloads/poker-event-planner.md`, and the two
affected entries in `_source/pages.json`. Both entries retain their October 8

search-section index, and existing reading-time references. All fifteen
production paths are bound to the exact published predecessor. The worksheet
preview still comes from the same file that is downloaded. Titles, descriptions,
source URLs, styles, scripts, other worksheets and public routes are unchanged.

There is no new account, form, submission, tracking event or runtime feature.
The new plan uses ordinary links and remains available without JavaScript.
