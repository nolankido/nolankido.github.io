Date: November 9, 2026

Baseline: `523ef1c34ea5ef88771b4b52de04cb40076e46e8`

## Observed issue

The optional `_tests/test_turn30_evidence_index_history.py` module had
five failed assertions. It expected a single later historical adapter to
rewind today's Start Here page, About page, page registry and sitemap to
their November 4 Turn 30 versions. Later reviewed website changes altered
those files, and the hash-gated predecessor appropriately refused to
replace unfamiliar modern content.

Unlike the Turn 29 ledger fixed in Turn 93, all six old and new blob
hashes recorded by the Turn 30 historical adapter match the exact
original commit and its direct parent. The defect was in the historical
test's use of current files as immutable release fixtures.

The related `_tests/test_turn30_evidence_types.py` also assumed the current
About page was still dated November 4. An independently approved About
revision advanced its metadata to November 6. Its sitemap comparison also
relied on current bytes being wholly rewound to the original Turn 30 release.

## Correction

- Pinned the Turn 30 reviewed release
  `0906e6ae2d51b062dd6d3bf34b86d6be6e432657` and its direct parent
  `c259d366176f3411e89777646135262178cc75b3`. Their Git tree hashes
  are `905851ef0b5a394c7dd65aeba1e518771c737e30` and
  `a88806d384ee754d06a25fb062d63ce0694dbbb8`.
- Used the existing read-only, hash-checked Git fixture reader to verify
  the original release and parent bytes for all six modified site files.
  Added exact provenance, predecessor, idempotence, path-alias and UTF-8
  checks without rewriting any live site bytes.
- Verified the page registry and sitemap rewind through original Turns
  30, 29 and 27 in chronological order, including the fixed Turn 29
  source-notes predecessor ledger.
- Verified new mutations, today's content, unrelated paths and binary
  inputs are preserved instead of being laundered into older history.
  Retained strict complete-record metadata rollback for Start Here.
- Repaired the companion evidence-type contract: its original one-URL
  sitemap-date change is now checked against hash-verified Turn 30 Git
  snapshots, while today's Start Here and About dates must match their
  current sitemap entries. The test no longer backdates About to 2026-11-04.

Only the two Turn 30 test modules and this public sprint report changed.
The live Start Here and About pages, indexed evidence, sitemap,
published dates, Person identity, site navigation and hosting are
unchanged. No GitHub Actions, cron, new automation or paid APIs were
introduced or used.
