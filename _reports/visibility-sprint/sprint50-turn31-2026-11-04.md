# Visibility sprint Turn 31: a source-bounded poker episode ledger

Reviewed November 4, 2026. Base website commit: `0906e6ae2d51b062dd6d3bf34b86d6be6e432657`.

## Decision

The next site improvement advances visibility for Nolan Kido as a real tournament poker player and practical AI user without fabricating footage or tournament facts. The original [Portland Meadows Episode 1 companion](https://nolankido.com/poker/episodes/portland-meadows-ep1/) already contains two substantial narrated hands and a carefully bounded payout distinction, but its exact chronology was checked against an archived version 7 edit, not the newer current public YouTube upload.

A new generic poker article or an unsourced Episode 2 transcript would dilute trust. This turn instead makes the **existing source-limited hand record useful to editors and viewers in a compact format**, with a downloadable proposed scene ledger that the site's already published offline checker can inspect. It gives future episodes a concrete proof format, not an invented outcome or a claim that the current cut was inspected.

## Public improvement

The Episode 1 companion has a new section, `#episode-evidence-ledger`, near the beginning, before the event summary and detailed hand reviews. It gives a readable three-item record:

1. **6-4 offsuit near the bubble.** The first-person account reports 13 remaining, 10 paid, 6-4 offsuit from the small blind shoving into A-K suited. The reported roughly 148,000 stack at a 15,000 big blind equals 9.8666 BB, shown as **about 9.87 BB** only if it was the pre-posting stack. Unknowns include individual suits, full board, effective stack, independently verified opponent range and strategy verdict.
2. **Pocket sevens four-handed.** The account reports 7-7 in the big blind, a button raise to 75,000 at a 30,000 big blind, a reshove, A-T offsuit calling, and the sevens holding. The reported 585,000 stack equals **19.5 BB**; the button raise-to amount equals **2.5 BB**. The full board, exact other stacks, payouts and a verified solver/ICM result are not available.
3. **Prize versus reported payment.** Advertised first prize **$5,100**; reported receipt from the four-way deal **$2,900**. The latter is a reported gross payment, not net tournament profit or a certified settlement.

These are not independent event-confirmation claims. The published episode is an author account with references to older narration and edit notes. No current-video timestamps or full boards were invented to make the story more dramatic.

## New public scene claims

[Download the bounded JSON ledger](../../downloads/portland-meadows-ep1-scene-claims.json).

The record is deliberately named a **conservative proposal for scene labels**. It contains exactly three `scenes` compatible with [the previously published fact gate](../../downloads/poker-vlog-fact-gate.py): `hand_64o`, `hand_77`, and `event_payout`. Its top-level fields state:

- Archived version 7 retrospective participant narration and notes, not a new video-accuracy audit.
- Current public-cut timestamps **not independently checked**.
- Current cut graphics **not independently checked**.
- Exact tournament calendar date **not independently verified**.
- Full board runouts **not established**.
- Human final-release approval **not established by this structured file**.

For both hand scenes the proposed labels omit individual suits and full boards, mark the reported stacks as approximate, and do not claim a verified solver verdict or reused old-cut timestamps. The payout scene keeps the two amounts separate and does not claim net profit.

The existing checker can be run locally:

The published companion also supplies the Windows `py -3` equivalents and links the reusable [editor worksheet](../../downloads/poker-vlog-truth-to-screen-qa.md).

### Checker scope

The earlier gate performs arithmetic and structured field comparisons against the *public first-person companion*. It cannot read video/audio, establish the true effective stacks or board runouts, verify a tournament organizer's payout, decide ICM, or prove a person's narration and graphics agree. A zero-targeted-flags result is not human sign-off on a released video. Its synthetic negative-control scenarios are deliberately constructed mistakes, not observed failures from this episode.

## Series progression

This is **progress on roadmap Turn 8, not completion of a fully sourced multi-episode series**. The existing [Poker hub](https://nolankido.com/poker/#watch) links three distinct published videos. Only Portland Meadows Episode 1 has this source-checked written companion, and even its current-cut timestamps remain unverified. The Evening Stand Off and WSOP Circuit Reserve Day 1 uploads still have no approved hand-by-hand source packet here.

The next candidate for a second original companion requires an approved current export or explicit episode source sheet containing exact voiceover, hand history, source status, missing suits, intended shown boards, chip/BB values, payout distinctions and current-cut scene timestamps. Once reviewed, it can use the same structured claim validation and audio/graphics acceptance sheet without conflating a story with a certified solver result. Do not invent those facts from the video titles.

Search relevance and visitor trust are plausible hypotheses, not measured outcomes. Actual public indexing and reader comprehension should be evaluated separately.
