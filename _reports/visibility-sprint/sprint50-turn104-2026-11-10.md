# Turn 104: Put Task Brief export after review

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `4ea76e1818654227279dbf00c89b5aaf890d5662`

## Observed reader problem

In a real Chrome session at **320 by 700 pixels**, the fictional meeting
example assembled into the correct six-section preview. At the end of that
preview, however, the **Copy brief** button began another **602 pixels**
below the preview's bottom edge. A 16-row duplicate plain-text field took
up most of the intervening screen. A reader who had finished reviewing the
structured brief had to pass the duplicate text before finding the actions.
These are observed interface measurements, not visitor analytics.

## Improvement

- Keep the six-section preview, its independent review reminder and the
  no-AI warning in place.
- Move the existing **Copy brief**, **Save as text**, **Print brief**, and
  **Select text manually** controls directly after the preview and its
  review instruction, before the duplicate plain-text field.
- Introduce a visible **Ready to keep a copy?** reminder to review all six
  sections before exporting. Label the actions as an accessible group using
  that reminder.
- Keep status feedback immediately after the buttons so copy, download and
  print messaging remains close to the action that triggered it.
- Retain the complete read-only plain-text field underneath the controls.
  Manual selection still focuses and selects that field. No export occurs
  automatically, and no JavaScript behavior, privacy boundary or print-only
  preview logic changed.
- Add three focused regression tests for the source and generated reading
  order, group labeling, explicit action boundaries, status feedback and print
  contract. Register them in the manual local release checklist.
