## Focused improvement

`_scripts/search_sections.py` now obtains standalone records through the existing `player_find.add` registry. That registry already validates each public page's source, canonical URL, identity, publication dates and indexability. The section builder reads only those exact registered public HTML files and uses the existing visible-heading parser. It does not scan arbitrary files or add a separate keyword list.

The builder rejects noncanonical routes, duplicate ownership, and collisions with any manifest route or generated output. This includes collisions with ineligible manifest pages. Its optional repository-root argument preserves the manifest-only behavior needed by minimal and historical fixtures. `_scripts/build.py` supplies the repository root for the actual site build.

The resulting index grows from **184 routes and 1,218 headings** to **189 routes and 1,258 headings**. The five additions are:

The serialized section payload grows from **61,975 to 64,946 UTF-8 bytes**, an increase of **2,971 bytes**. All 184 previous routes retain identical decoded heading rows. All **189 native result cards remain byte-for-byte unchanged**. Their concatenated markup retains SHA-256 `4e6d95e75ae7e69785655282675bc2642eba0ebbfa75f4ae98fcd72963a6bc84`.

No JavaScript is changed. Search still runs locally in the browser, with at most three matching section links per card. Ordinary browsing continues to work without JavaScript. Find retains `noindex, follow`; this turn does not create another externally indexable search-results surface.

## Enlarged-text repair found during testing

The new browser journeys exposed an existing Find layout defect. At a 320-pixel viewport with 200% root text size and additional text spacing, both the baseline and the initial updated page widened to 322 pixels. The `Search results.` heading exceeded its column, and the fixed NK mark was too small for its enlarged text.

Two rules in `assets/site-find.css`, both scoped to `.page-find`, allow the content column to wrap and the mobile NK mark to grow with its text. The final enlarged-text Find views stay within the tested 320- and 390-pixel viewports. This does not change article layouts or shared stylesheets. The ordinary result cards are preserved.

`find/index.html` is the only changed HTML file. Its differences are limited to the section-index JSON and the versioned Find stylesheet URL, now `/assets/site-find.css?v=1ccbeb6438b4`. No article copy, authorship, credentials, titles, descriptions, publication dates, review dates, JSON-LD, canonical URLs, sitemap entries or feeds changed.
