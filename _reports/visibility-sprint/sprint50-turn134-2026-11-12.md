# Turn 134: Connect wine and games to the public AI-and-judgment story

Date: November 12, 2026

Baseline: `0863e5ab6ee831a11fabef6ad8ad163e4268f74b`

## Observed gap

The homepage's **Beyond the screen** paragraph mentioned food, travel and
learning, but not wine tasting or game-making. About used the generic heading
**Outside the categories** for those interests. Public poker and AI examples
were already linked; no public game release, wine credential, or evaluated
wine-AI project is in this repository. Visitors therefore had no on-site
description of these two interests and how they relate to the stated principle
**AI Works, Human Decides**.

This is an observable editorial and reader-navigation gap, not a measured
ranking opportunity or evidence of search demand.

## Improvement

- The homepage now names wine tasting and making games as personal interests
  alongside food, travel, and learning, with one native link to a matching
  explanation on About. The existing public poker record route stays intact.
- About now has a specific **Wine, games, and the rest of life** heading at
  `#wine-games-and-life`. The short explanation distinguishes first-hand
  tasting and playtesting from possible AI assistance, with limits on what is
  claimed. It does not assert a wine qualification, a published game or a
  verified AI performance outcome.
- The section remains within the current About profile, rather than adding
  an unsubstantiated credential to the Person schema or publishing private
  prototype names or links. No new public project, article, domain or external
  account is claimed.
- A scoped `:target` outline makes the destination visible on a normal
  homepage-to-About fragment arrival. Existing native scroll offsets and
  reduced-motion-compatible behavior were retained.
- The existing source-driven Find directory automatically gained the unique
  About section heading and its direct fragment, so visitors can find the
  topic locally without a new search service.
- No homepage or About publication/modified dates, canonical URLs, search
  descriptions, author IDs, news-feed entries or existing evidence links
  changed. No GitHub Actions, recurring process, tracker or paid API was added.
