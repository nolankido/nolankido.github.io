# Turn 40: connect recently published poker videos to Nolan's canonical site

Reviewed November 5, 2026. Starting `main` commit: `e71ee6795f2886664d7e2117df229c6e6a2166ad`.

## Why this turn

The Poker hub and tournament player profile said that only three public videos were available. Nolan subsequently shared two more links in his Portland Meadows publishing conversations: an Episode 3 upload and a Day 4 upload. The site had not yet connected those videos to Nolan's established poker presence and public identity. A visitor looking for his latest first-person tournament work would see an outdated selection.

The videos were identified from their **owner-supplied public URLs**, not from an independently inspected final cut, a YouTube metadata API or a third-party search result. Search tools did not return a reliable video page for either new ID. Therefore the website uses neutral episode/day labels and avoids exact current YouTube titles, payouts, hand details, card suits, strategies or timestamps that cannot be verified from the available evidence.
