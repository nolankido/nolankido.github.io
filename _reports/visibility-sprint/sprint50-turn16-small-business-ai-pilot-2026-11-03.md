## Published improvement

[Technology: How can a small business start using AI on a real workflow?](https://nolankido.com/technology/#small-business-ai-pilot)

1. Choose a bounded, reversible task and verify data permission before using AI.
2. Brief the accepted facts, forbidden promises, named reviewer and stop conditions.
3. Establish a quality baseline and keep an accountable trial record with approved or fictional inputs.
4. Independently check content and have a person approve or reject use.
5. Count setup, review, repairs, upkeep and failures before deciding whether to continue.
