## Correction 1: Stand Off payout format is material to both decisions

The A-K example previously said that the tournament objective was
"not established precisely" and named a missing payout ladder, but it
did not explain the unusual event structure already documented in
the Stand Off companion.

The source says the tournament stopped after **twelve rounds** and
paid players still holding chips at the stop, with their chip holdings
used in an **ICM-based payout calculation**. A source-packet description
of a camera clock places the hand in **round 10 of 12 with 17 players
remaining from 78 entries**, at **3,000 / 6,000 / 6,000**. These are
*narrated and archived-source claims*, not official rule verification.

The example now explains:

- Preserving approximately 46,000 chips by folding at decision B would
  preserve those chips **at that moment**, but could not guarantee
  survival to the scheduled stop.
- Calling against two all-in players could improve the chip count or
  terminate the possibility of reaching the stop.
- Both the eligibility/survival condition and the chip-based prize
  distribution matter. A conventional fixed-place payout ladder or
  an unrelated final-table ICM chart cannot be substituted for the
  event's unverified formula.
- Exact player stacks, the payout computation, any exceptions and
  independently certified rules remain unknown. No optimal call/fold
  conclusion is offered.

This repair is confined to the same public Markdown A-K review. It
does not alter the archived Stand Off source companion or invent a
new payout schedule.
