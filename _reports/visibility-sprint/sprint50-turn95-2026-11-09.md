## Observed issue

The optional `_tests/test_turn31_episode_ledger_history.py` module had
four failed assertions. It expected the current Portland Meadows Episode
1 companion, player sitemap builder, public GET checker and sitemap to
reconstruct the Turn 31 release after only two later predecessors.
Approved changes after November 4 altered those files. Exact hash-gated
predecessors correctly preserved unfamiliar, later content.

using today's bytes as if they were frozen release fixtures.

the original sitemap's URLs with today's sitemap after one late adapter.

comparison obsolete. These were legitimate new URLs, not an Episode 1
ledger regression.

  original hashes, predecessor restoration, idempotence, path aliases
  and UTF-8 text views.
- Added a sitemap chain regression that rewinds the exact original
  releases in order through Turns 31, 30, 29 and 27. Later site
  revisions and partial or unrecognized edits are not rewritten.
- Pinned the newly published public Episode 1 scene-claims download to

  snapshots, while the current sitemap is checked against the current
  Episode 1 revision date and the deterministic local builder. RSS
