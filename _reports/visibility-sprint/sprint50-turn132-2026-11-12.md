# Turn 132: Connect three AI case studies to the author's public evidence

Date: November 12, 2026

Baseline: `a31816df4201ea943302a59c6dcde3404658ea8c`

## Observed reader-path issue

The About page already introduces Nolan Kido's research, practical AI work,
and first-person poker records. It points directly to Review Reply Writer,
Preflight, and the AI-assisted poker-video evidence case. Those three case
articles did not, within their article body, give an incoming reader a
contextual way to inspect the author's background and the different kinds of
public evidence. Their generic site navigation did link About, but it did not
explain the relevance of that route to a source-minded reader.

This is an observed document-navigation gap, not a measured trust, traffic,
conversion, search-ranking, or AI-answer-citation problem.

## Improvement

- Added one visible, native **About the author** paragraph after the evidence
  limitations in each of three existing articles:
  `/technology/local-ai-review-replies/`,
  `/technology/ai-book-continuity-checker/`, and
  `/technology/ai-poker-video-evidence/`.
- Each link goes directly to `/about/#about-evidence-title`, the published
  section distinguishing peer-reviewed research, bounded practical AI
  examples, and poker records. About already links back to all three cases.
- Kept the invitation honest. The Review Reply Writer route does not claim
  customer benefit or time savings; the Preflight route does not claim
  accuracy, author savings or paying customers; the poker editing route
  does not certify the current video cut or independently verify recollections.
- Used the site's existing `small-copy` style, native HTML and stable About
  fragment. No redesign, JavaScript, tracking, new schema, unpublished claim,
  date change, feed edit, URL change, extra network request, scheduled job or
  GitHub Actions workflow was added.
- Added four focused regression tests for source and published HTML,
  reciprocal routes, the precise About anchor, bounded claims, unchanged
  dates, deterministic builds and deliberately broken or duplicated links.
  Registered the suite in the manual release checklist.
