# Turn 150: Give NolanPoker's public video gallery real thumbnails

Date: November 13, 2026 (Pacific)

Baseline: `132e2694f175d59b2f4efb89ec7d76bc8d511d8b` (Turn 149 report release)

## Observed gap

The NolanPoker section on the public Poker hub was a text-only list even
though all six linked YouTube uploads had published thumbnail artwork. A
visitor could scan the titles but could not visually recognize the video
subjects before leaving the site. Two Portland Meadows cards also used
generic episode labels rather than the current public upload titles. The
prior turn had already improved the order and readability of the text cards.
No change in visitor engagement was assumed.

## Changes

- Checked YouTube's public oEmbed metadata on November 13 for all six linked
  uploads, including the associated NolanPoker channel, current displayed
  title and available thumbnail. This metadata check did not inspect the
  video footage, narration, graphics, timestamps or tournament claims.
- Saved six authentic 480 x 360 JPEG thumbnails locally, totaling **101,444
  bytes**, under `assets/poker/video-thumbnails/`. The page serves these
  local files rather than hotlinking thumbnail images from YouTube.
- Added each thumbnail directly inside its existing accessible video link.
  Images use empty alternative text because the linked title supplies the
  accessible name. Loading is lazy and decoding is asynchronous. A decorative
  play badge communicates that the card opens a video without introducing an
  iframe, automatic playback or additional script.
- Used a 16:9 cropped presentation, `object-fit: cover`, consistent responsive
  card styling and keyboard focus indication. The underlying files remain
  intact and are not artificially upscaled.
- Replaced the generic Episode 3 and Day 4 gallery labels with their verified
  public YouTube titles, and synchronized the source-bounded player profile.
- Added `_source/poker/nolanpoker-video-thumbnails.json` with the six source
  locations, check date and content SHA-256 hashes. Claims are explicitly
  limited to metadata and thumbnail provenance.
- Updated the relevant Poker content dates to November 13, rebuilt affected
  deterministic pages, and added four Turn 150 regression tests covering
  image integrity, link accessibility, metadata boundaries and styling.

## Evidence boundary and next opportunity

This turn verifies the published artwork, titles, link semantics, layout,
provenance boundaries and source integrity. It does **not** independently
verify the underlying videos, establish the accuracy of hand claims, or prove
an uplift in YouTube click-through rate, dwell time, SEO, GEO visibility or
subscriber growth. The next improvement should make the gallery's selection
guidance more useful to a first-time poker viewer, using visitor-facing
clarity and accessible navigation without adding unapproved tracking.
