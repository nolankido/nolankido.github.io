# Turn 145: Give the Review Reply Writer evaluation a clear evidence path

Date: November 12, 2026

Baseline: `70dbb3b91669074dee00ad3ca3f9fe7770903a6e`

## Observed problem

The Review Reply Writer case explains a 30-case, offline evaluation across
multiple sections. Its top-of-page project shortcuts reach the example,
failure, workflow and sources, but readers arriving at the evaluation
cannot immediately choose between the recorded template counts, earlier
Gemma outputs, repeatable method and limits. After the table, the
qualification about drafts and safety is easy to pass without opening
the separate method and limitations sections.

This is a navigation and evidence-context gap, not a measurement of
reader failure or a claim that a better route improves rankings.

## Improvement

- Add a short, named four-link navigation inside the evaluation overview:
  recorded template counts, earlier Gemma runs, repeat the offline check,
  and what remains unproven. Every destination is an existing heading.
- Add a contextual route immediately after the 30-case count interpretation
  that points directly to the method and limitations, where the numbers
  receive their necessary qualification.
- At the end of the limits section, link back to the repeatable method
  and forward to the existing project sources.
- Reuse the already-scoped responsive jump-link styling, its 44-pixel
  targets and the existing fragment-arrival handling. No new JavaScript,
  third-party dependency, remote API, CI, GitHub Actions or automation.
- Regenerate the source-owned public page. Leave results, references,
  source links, canonical URL, published date, author identity and
  structured-data claims unchanged.
- Add four dependency-free regression tests, including mutation controls,
  and register them in the local release checklist.
