# Turn 149: Put NolanPoker video choices before the source ledger

Date: November 12, 2026 (Pacific)

Baseline: `7096b448f2c423b081f682dc0f0b364257ba6ad8` (Turn 148 release receipt)

## Observed gap

On the public Poker hub at a 320 px viewport, the first public video card
started approximately **1,397 px below the NolanPoker section start**.
A long verification paragraph appeared before the gallery. The video titles
were `h4` elements, while the relevant card typography styled `h3`, leaving
the video title at browser-default **16 px**. That hindered choosing a video,
especially on a narrow screen. No increase in traffic, search visibility or
view duration was assumed.

## Changes

- Kept all six existing public video cards, video destinations, companion
  article links and individual claim boundaries.
- Replaced the long introductory source paragraph with a short explanation
  and an accessible `#nolanpoker-video-notes` jump link.
- Placed the existing detailed source ledger below the video grid, under a
  visible heading. Its distinctions between independently listed results,
  archived-source review, owner-supplied links and audiovisual verification
  remain intact.
- Retained the independent tournament player record link after the gallery.
- Applied page-scoped `h4` styling, responsive title size, explicit 44 px
  minimum video-title link height, more visible focus indication and a
  restrained top border on the first card.
- Rebuilt the Poker hub, library, tournament player profile and both Portland
  Meadows companion pages so shared CSS version hashes remain reproducible.
- Added three regression tests with negative controls for heading styling,
  focus states, explanatory copy order, source-note links and claim caveats.
- Preserved the November 12 existing content modification date. No artificial
  freshness date or new facts were introduced.
