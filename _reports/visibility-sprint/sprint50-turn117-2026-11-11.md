# Turn 117: Put the answer-checking step beside the completed brief

Date: November 11, 2026

Baseline: `ce72b6f3c4f334b3c079234357528fbdaaf4942a`

## Reader problem

After reviewing and exporting a task brief, the next actionable link was
below the **16-row plain-text textarea**. That field has a minimum height
of 340 CSS pixels and becomes substantially taller with enlarged reader
text. A reader returning from the export buttons could miss the next step
or have to scroll through the entire selectable text field to find it.
The guide itself already worked and the same-page fragment was stable;
the problem was the placement of the link.
Connected Chrome confirmed the baseline gap: at a 320px-wide viewport,
the old link began **1,041px below the Save as text button**, with no
horizontal overflow. The measured default root font was 16px, so this
was a narrow-screen observation, not a 200% enlargement measurement.

## Targeted change

- Move the existing `#brief-result-next` link directly after the export
  controls and status, **before** the lengthy copy/save explanation and
  long textarea.
  This is a single moved link, not an additional route or action.
- Make the handoff visibly distinct with a narrow green rule and wrapping
  text. The global keyboard focus ring still applies to the native link.
- Instruct readers to **confirm that a saved or copied brief exists** before
  choosing the independent check. Explain that the check section is on the
  same page, so a reader can visit it without losing unsaved builder fields.
- Keep the six-part preview, copy, download, print, fallback selections,
  plain-text contents and existing answer-checking guide unchanged.
- Advance the task-brief modification date to November 11, 2026 in the
  page's JSON-LD and sitemap. Historical date guards now allow a newer
  modification date rather than pinning this page to the prior day.
- Extend the optional offline browser harness to follow the native link
  with **200% reader text**, keyboard Enter, 320/390/620px widths and an
  assembled fictional brief. It checks link placement, fragment arrival,
  sticky-header clearance, lack of horizontal overflow and preservation
  of the assembled text. Add three local Turn 117 regressions for the
  source and built markup, focus styling and handoff contracts.
