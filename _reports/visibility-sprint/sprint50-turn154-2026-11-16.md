Scope: Two already published, manually built written companions

## Reader problem

Turn 153 introduced a source-limited hand-to-study route on the Poker homepage,
but a reader arriving directly at either written companion would not see
those specific matches at the decision being reviewed. Episode 1 already
had generic follow-up study links, including a broad 10–20 BB guide for
its approximately 9.87 BB 6-4 offsuit hand. The Stand Off discussion
also had a general short-stack link later in the article, but not within
the immediate second-decision section.

## Implemented content

In the **Portland Meadows Episode 1** article:

- At `#hand-64o-record`, changed the first follow-up from the generic
  short-stack guide to the already published 6–10 BB tournament guide.
  Repeated the important limitation that its worked examples do not
  solve this exact, source-limited 6-4 hand.
- At `#hand-77-record`, named the existing 10–20 BB study destination
  explicitly. Clarified that it is a framework rather than a validated
  opponent range for the approximately 19.5 BB reshove.
- Each section now returns directly to the three-source-hand study
  index at `/poker/#nolanpoker-hand-study`.

In the separate **Portland Meadows Stand Off** companion:

- Added a short follow-up immediately after the decision facing the
  two shoves at `#standoff-second-decision`.
- Linked the existing 10–20 BB tournament framework and back to the
  three-hand study index.
- Explicitly separated the roughly 10.2 BB **pre-flat** stack from
  the approximately 46,000 chips left facing two shoves. The guide
  cannot establish that either a call or a fold was optimal.

No source supplies a confirmed 3–5 BB hand for these three situations.
Neither companion now makes such a claim. All publication narrative,
missing-board warnings, payout distinctions, source limitations,
YouTube URLs, and original hand anchors are preserved.
