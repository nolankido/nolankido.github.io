# Turn 110: Release the Task Brief's sticky header at extreme reflow

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `608b4884182c2d445d42a27c779c1b3c372ef127`

## Observed reader problem

On the live Task Brief Builder at **160 by 700 CSS pixels**, the wrapped
navigation remained sticky and covered **304 pixels** of the viewport
(43%). With the complete fictional meeting brief selected, its label
started at **324 pixels**, its copy cue ended at **545 pixels**, and the
actual selected text began at **557 pixels**. Only the bottom **143
pixels** were available for reading selected text.

This is a useful extreme-reflow stress case, approximating the effective
CSS width of a 320-pixel screen at high page zoom. **It is not a
measurement of native 200% browser zoom or text-only scaling.**

## Improvement

- At **240 CSS pixels or narrower**, let the Task Brief's site header
  scroll out of view instead of remaining sticky. This frees reading
  space after the reader moves into the builder or selected text.
- Match the non-sticky state with **20 pixels** of native anchor scroll
  padding and no extra per-section scroll margin. Bookmarks should no
  longer reserve room for a header that is offscreen.
- Keep the normal sticky navigation and larger anchor margins at widths
  above 240 pixels. This is scoped to the Task Brief page and does not
  change other pages.
- Preserve the first-line selection reset, complete selected range,
  manual-copy cue and accessibility description. Add three regressions
  for scoped CSS, the selected-text scroll calculation with an
  offscreen header, and fingerprinted generated output.
