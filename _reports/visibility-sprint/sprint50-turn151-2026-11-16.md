# Turn 151: Help new NolanPoker viewers choose a first video

Date: November 16, 2026 (Pacific)

Baseline: \`8ed08cb1d39ab02f59982a77841acb23bb38cc07\` (latest main at start)

## Observed visitor problem

Turn 150 made six public YouTube cards recognizable with authentic, locally
served thumbnails. Visitors still had to read every title to decide where to
begin. The first card was a South Point visit by list order, not necessarily
the starting point a newcomer wanted. No engagement or preference data was
used to infer an ideal video.

## One bounded improvement

- Added one native, labeled "New here? Start with" navigation above the gallery.
  Its three destination choices are a short-stack comeback, an A-K decision,
  and a casino visit. All are grounded in existing public card titles and
  their accompanying source notes.
- Linked each choice to a unique fragment on the existing matching video card.
  The links do not open a video or send data to YouTube until someone actively
  selects the video itself.
- Added accessible 44-pixel-minimum choice targets, visible keyboard focus,
  responsive wrapping, target highlighting, and scroll offset that avoids the
  fixed site header.
- Shortened the pre-gallery explanation to keep the first video close to the
  choices. Preserved the verification link and explicit "not official results"
  boundary.
- Preserved the six YouTube URLs, original order, thumbnails, source-ledger
  caveats, independently supported tournament-record links and existing
  written companions. No new video facts, footage claims, tracking, scripts,
  CI, schedules or paid services were introduced.
- Advanced only the Poker hub's actual content revision date to November 16.
  The separate player profile and November 13 thumbnail check retain their
  own review dates.

## Verification boundary and subsequent opportunity

This change establishes a clearer set of visitor choices and preserves
working, source-bounded internal destinations. It does not establish a
measurable improvement to YouTube views, subscriptions, search rankings,
AI-assistant citations or visitor satisfaction. A later check should confirm
live fragment navigation and mobile focus behavior in a connected browser.
Selection guidance should only be adjusted based on actual visitor observation
or authorized first-party data, not a guessed preferred watch order.
