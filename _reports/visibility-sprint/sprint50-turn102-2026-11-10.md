# Turn 102: Show the beginning of a loaded task brief

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `b4d6f79824994cab27deb17f8483b1b68541db0f`

## Observed reader problem

A real browser inspection of the published `/technology/task-brief/` page
at a **320 by 700 pixel viewport** confirmed the new meeting starter was
visible, keyboard-operable and did not create horizontal page overflow.
Pressing Enter on its button loaded all five fields and focused the first
textarea. But that field's caret landed at character **113 of 113**, and the
textarea had `scrollTop` near **49 pixels**. The visitor saw the end of the
example first, not its opening purpose. This was directly observed in the
browser, not inferred from CSS.

The same loader powers the six original example buttons and replacement
confirmation. Leaving the caret at the end made a long example harder to
review from its beginning, especially on a small screen.

## Improvement

- After successful example loading, focus the first textarea, place the caret
  at offset zero, and reset that textarea's vertical scroll to zero.
- Apply the behavior to the new meeting quick-start button and all six
  existing example controls, including a confirmed replacement.
- Preserve the safe replacement confirmation, cancel behavior, existing
  field contents, validation, and ability to assemble or save a brief.
- Keep unchanged the site's actual content modification date, because this
  is a behavior repair and not a substantive editorial revision.
- Add a focused synthetic-DOM test that deliberately emulates a browser
  focusing at the end of a long textarea and verifies the corrected opening
  position after first load and both replacement routes. Update the earlier
  synthetic DOM fixture to expose the native textarea selection method.
