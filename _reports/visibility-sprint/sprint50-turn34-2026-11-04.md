## Why this turn

The active [visibility, credibility and identity strategy](../identity-visibility-strategy-2026-11-04.md) calls for useful public evidence, accurate search-facing descriptions, readable pages and local release checks. Turn 33 added a second Portland Meadows written companion. Before adding more content, a complete checkout revealed that the existing release was not reproducible and some newer pages were absent from validation coverage.

This turn repairs those observed problems. It does not count a publication or a passing checker as evidence of search rankings, AI citations, visitor satisfaction or verified video footage.

## Scope

The public content changes are the notes case's generated outline and reading estimate, plus command-block layouts in the first poker companion, the laptop guide and the reply-audit article. Existing route names, the shared Person identity, original article publication dates, tournament accounts, source limitations, video URLs and feed identities remain intact. The Stand Off article's current-video verification boundary remains explicit.

The local build and audit repairs affect maintainer scripts and tests. They do not add a site service, a remote search API, a visitor-data collection mechanism or a new public article. All work is performed locally or through manually invoked read-only public requests. No GitHub Actions, scheduled jobs or paid APIs are introduced.
