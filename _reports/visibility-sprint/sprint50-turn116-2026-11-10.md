# Turn 116: Keep the selected brief visible on short mobile screens

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `c6fe47c94537455f72811bcc211307e0e1a63f35`

## Observed reader problem

The previous turn put the Save and Print alternatives beside a selected
task brief. A real connected Chrome inspection of the published page
showed the unintended cost on a short mobile screen. At **320 by 568
CSS pixels**, after selecting the brief:

- The sticky header ended at **153px**.
- The fallback alternatives occupied **292px** of vertical space.
- The text field started at **602px**, entirely below the **568px**
  viewport. No part of the selected brief was visible.
- The document did not overflow horizontally. The problem was vertical
  visibility at the exact moment the reader needed to copy or save.

## Improvement

- Shorten the visible nearby export labels to **Save .txt** and
  **Print / PDF**, retaining complete and descriptive `aria-label`
  values for assistive technology.
- Tighten the fallback panel's explanation, spacing, padding and button
  gap while preserving a 44px minimum touch target and the warning that
  requesting an export does not confirm a saved file.
- After selecting the brief, check the actual viewport height and the
  geometry of the selected text and first still-available fallback
  action. If the text would be below the fold, adjust vertical scrolling
  just enough to reveal its beginning, constrained so the first fallback
  action remains below the sticky header.
- Keep focus and the full native text selection unchanged. The previous
  label-first position remains in use when the viewport has sufficient
  space, and the new correction is skipped when geometry APIs or a
  fallback action are absent.
- Add two Turn 116 regression tests for short and tall viewport
  behavior, action visibility, preservation of selection, semantic
  labels, responsive CSS, deterministic asset versions and local-only
  behavior. Update the Turn 115 wording assertion and register this
  turn in the existing local manual release checklist.
