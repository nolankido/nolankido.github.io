# Turn 112: Warn when the full manual-copy selection is lost

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `4504840a844bf2f69c6cbe1a3dec8d05b6e674c7`

## Observed reader problem

The Task Brief Builder selects every character when a reader chooses
**Select text manually**. If the reader then clicks in the textarea,
uses an arrow key, or adjusts a touch selection, the selected range may
become shorter. The visible cue and status could still imply that the
**complete** brief was selected.

Turn 111's native Copy handler intentionally ignored incomplete ranges,
so a reader trying to copy only part of the brief might receive no
correction and unknowingly leave with an incomplete prompt.

## Improvement

- Track native textarea selection, keyboard-release and pointer-release
  events only while the manual-copy cue is active.
- When the complete selection is lost, show **"Full brief no longer
  selected. Choose Select text manually again."** beside the text.
  Update the polite status once per transition, avoiding repeated
  announcements on every arrow key.
- When all characters are selected again, restore the selected-only
  cue and warn that selection alone still does not mean copying.
- If a native Copy command occurs with only part of the brief selected,
  warn that the attempted copy may be incomplete and recommend
  reselecting the complete text. Never prevent the browser's Copy
  command or claim that it succeeded.
- Preserve reset behavior after editing, rebuilding, automatic copy,
  printing and downloading. Ignore copy or selection events while the
  manual cue is hidden.
- Add two local regressions covering actual production handlers,
  partial/complete transitions, non-interception of native Copy, live
  status changes, and generated-asset integrity. Update the earlier
  native-copy regression to expect this stronger incomplete-range
  warning.
