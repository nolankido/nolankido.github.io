# Turn 127: Make independent AI evidence limits visible on arrival

Date: November 11, 2026

Baseline: `7cfd638a8e67a109c61010a9ef25c007e2963ff1`

## Reader problem

The independently maintained local-AI laptop guide and independent reply
audit had accurate source limitations, but important distinctions were spread
across long introductory paragraphs and later sections. A reader who entered
at the article, skimmed its first screen, and left could miss that the public
timings came from a different computer or mistake a narrow lexical screen
for business approval. This is an inspectable information-order issue, not a
claimed result from analytics or an observed usability study.

## Improvement

- Added a short, labeled **Evidence at a glance** summary immediately before
  each article's section navigation. Native descriptions distinguish observed
  results, their origin, known misses, and unmeasured outcomes.
- The laptop guide says explicitly that no new MSI laptop timing, energy,
  or whole-task savings measurement was produced. Historical Gemma timings
  remain attributed to the GitHub-hosted Ubuntu CPU runner. Its summary
  leads to the original evidence section and bounded loopback test.
- The independent reply audit distinguishes three unflagged archived replies,
  three flagged deliberately damaged controls, and four further synthetic
  boundary probes with two missed offers and one flagged quotation. It
  explicitly rejects the inference that screening implies human approval,
  production accuracy, customer success, or a new model run.
- Added one local, content-versioned stylesheet for the two standalone pages.
  The native `<aside>` and `<dl>` structure has narrow-screen single-column
  fallback and printable styling. Neither page requires JavaScript for the
  new evidence summary.
- Added a four-test offline claim, route, layout-asset and date regression,
  with deliberately misleading-summary and broken-link negative controls.
  Updated the existing standalone-reader stylesheet fixture to include the
  unchanged new stylesheet when testing independent CSS cache versions.
  An offline Chromium browser regression was authored for future environments
  where the executable can launch.

The two articles retain their original sources, canonical URLs, author
identity, publication and revision dates, historical records, source-linked
downloads, and existing section bookmarks. No feed, sitemap, analytics,
external service, paid API, scheduled task, or GitHub Actions workflow was
changed. No new trial or measured search outcome is claimed.
