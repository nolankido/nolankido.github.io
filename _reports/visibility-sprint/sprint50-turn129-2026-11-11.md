# Turn 129: A direct mobile route to the evidence summaries

Date: November 11, 2026

Baseline: `a0428039c0d4394823837ed13f50c8a7e3085047`

## Observed reader problem

Turn 128 made two independent AI article summaries visible earlier on desktop.
The public mobile review at 390 by 844 pixels nevertheless placed the reply
audit summary at 871 pixels, just below the first viewport. On smaller phones,
the long article heading and site navigation take additional vertical space.
A reader who needs the evidence limits first should have a native route to the
summary without hunting for it. This observation does not establish a
measured bounce rate, search ranking problem or visitor preference.

## Improvement

- Added a small **Jump to evidence summary** link inside the article hero of
  both independently compiled case pages. It appears on screens up to 760
  pixels wide, immediately after the author attribution. Both links use
  existing article heading IDs and native URL fragments, so no JavaScript,
  tracking or alternative route is needed.
- Kept desktop hero layouts unchanged by hiding the small shortcut above the
  mobile breakpoint. The link's touch target is at least 44 pixels tall.
- Applied a scroll offset to the linked summary heading so the sticky site
  navigation does not cover the heading after a native fragment jump. A
  target outline helps readers recognize their destination. The existing
  global reduced-motion policy still disables smooth scrolling when requested.
- Added focused tests for the exact target, generated output, versioned CSS,
  unchanged article canonical and revision dates, plus negative fixtures for
  duplicate, delayed, or misdirected links. Registered the tests in the
  manual local release checklist.

No article claims, evidence facts, headings, URLs, publication dates, feeds,
sitemap contents, privacy settings, external services, scheduled jobs or
GitHub Actions workflows were changed. The source prose remains the source
of truth; the hero shortcut belongs to each standalone page builder.
