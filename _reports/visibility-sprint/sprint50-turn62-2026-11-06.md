# Turn 62: Keep the two main reading gateways usable with enlarged text

Date: November 6, 2026

Baseline: `281e9aac0d151f5dc47dde013131509596e3845d`

## Observed reader problem

The [Start Here](https://nolankido.com/start-here/) and [Writing](https://nolankido.com/notes/) pages are important routes from Nolan Kido's introduction to his practical AI, poker, and creative work. Both fit an ordinary 320-pixel viewport, but an independent local Chromium sweep found horizontal overflow with 200% root text and increased letter, word and line spacing:

| Page | Baseline document width | Reviewed width after fix | Viewport |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `/start-here/` | 376 pixels | 320 pixels | 320 pixels |
| `/notes/` | 387 pixels | 320 pixels | 320 pixels |

The Start Here overflow came from long section headings, including **Choose the direction that fits** and **Three different ways in**. The Writing overflow included long linked essay titles in the interest routes. Without a fix, a reader enlarging text could lose the right end of those lines or scroll the entire page sideways. Visual browser inspection also found crowded, sometimes overlapping mobile navigation labels on both pages, and native section jumps could place their heading behind the sticky header.

## Focused correction

- In the existing `entrypoints.css`, extend the page-scoped section wrapping rule to `.page-start-here .section-content`. The earlier Resources correction is preserved.
- In the existing `author-archive.css`, allow `.page-notes .section-content` to wrap long words in linked titles and supporting text.
- On these two pages only, let mobile navigation wrap into readable flex rows rather than squeezing long labels into narrow grid columns. Keep every label present and keyboard-reachable, and preserve the remaining site's navigation layout.
- Give these pages' established section fragment targets a `scroll-margin-top` large enough to clear the sticky header when text is enlarged. This includes the Writing topic links and Start Here reading routes.

The rules use `overflow-wrap: anywhere` so browser text can reflow naturally. They do not hide overflow, shrink accessible text, alter content, or change the shared layout for unrelated pages.

Both stylesheets receive new content-derived cache versions. The deterministic builder regenerates **nine public HTML pages**, changing only the versioned stylesheet URL in each one: Start Here, Writing, Resources, and six original Notes essays. A byte-for-byte comparison after normalizing those two version tokens confirms all nine otherwise match the baseline. The essays' published dates, their body text and structured data, all outgoing links, canonical URLs, the Person ID, the sitemap, and RSS feeds remain unchanged.

This is a reader-accessibility and presentation repair. It makes existing named work easier to reach and read, but does not establish improved search ranking, indexing, AI-answer citations, or reader retention.
