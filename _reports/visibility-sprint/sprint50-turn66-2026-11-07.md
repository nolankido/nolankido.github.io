# Turn 66: Keep Nolan Kido's Living Well hub readable at enlarged text

Date: November 7, 2026

Baseline: `9788a8bfcb99f0e68b6f4f9940888f11be15d460`

## Observed problem

The Living Well hub introduces Nolan Kido's work on meaning, attention, relationships and useful technology. Its existing essays, worksheets and reading routes connect these interests with the author. This turn addresses the presentation of that existing material, not new claims or content.

At a 320-pixel viewport with a 200% root text size and additional line, character and word spacing, the original page widened to **397 pixels with its committed fonts** and **413 pixels with fallback fonts**. The introductory text exceeded its reading column. Several primary navigation labels also exceeded their fixed grid cells, and some section labels overflowed their narrow metadata columns at wider viewports. These are reproducible offline layout measurements, not traffic or analytics.

The shared Living Well stylesheet serves 76 pages. Changing that shared asset for this hub-only problem would unnecessarily alter the other 75 pages.

## Focused improvement

- Add `assets/living-well-reader.css`, scoped to the exact `.page-living-well` body class and loaded only on the hub.
- Allow long text in the hub's reading and metadata columns to wrap within the available space.
- At widths up to 620 pixels, let the six original primary navigation links wrap naturally. Preserve their wording, order and destinations, and let the NK mark grow with enlarged text.
- Add mobile scroll clearance to the main content and existing section targets so native fragment arrivals clear the taller sticky header.
- Register the versioned stylesheet in the deterministic generator and the read-only public-byte verification inventory.
- Add a focused local browser regression and a unit contract that enforces hub-only ownership, the stylesheet content hash and exact expected live-target bytes.

No source copy, author identity, credentials, SEO titles, descriptions, dates, JSON-LD, resource lists, sitemap entries or feeds were changed. The generated `living-well/index.html` differs only by its new versioned stylesheet link. Removing that link restores the baseline HTML byte-for-byte. Independent review found no other changed generated output and confirmed that the other 75 Living Well pages remain unchanged. The existing content modification date is preserved rather than advanced for a styling change.
