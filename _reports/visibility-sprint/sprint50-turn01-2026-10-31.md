## 5. Baseline conclusions and next-turn contract

1. **No mass technical SEO rewrite is indicated by the static audit.** Turn 2 should hunt for actual live issues and exceptions in high-value pages, not spend hours rewriting already unique titles.
2. **Identity consistency needs inspection.** Turn 3 should reconcile two forms of Person data and validate every profile attribute against published evidence and owner privacy preferences.
3. **The largest clear content gap is Nolan's real poker history.** Turn 5 should build a dated player profile with independently linked results. Turn 6 should connect genuinely published videos to on-site editorial companions.
4. **Practical AI already has meaningful examples, but firsthand computing and general software work deserve flagships.** Turns 8 to 10 should privilege reproducible cases and public project artifacts over generic essays.
5. **Actual search gains remain to be observed.** Turn 15 should compare settled pre/post periods at 30 and 90 days without making causal or statistical claims that the data cannot support.
