# Turn 114: Give concrete manual-copy instructions on every device

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `1d56283e486b0af87c9eb0f174bfae909df80ce6`

## Reader problem

The Task Brief Builder offered a visible manual selection and accurate
success limits, but told readers only to use their **device's Copy
command**. Someone unfamiliar with copying selected text, especially
while switching between keyboard and touch, still had to guess the
actual next interaction. That was an avoidable point of friction at
the final handoff.

## Improvement

- Replace the short selected-text cue with an actionable instruction:
  **Selected, not copied. Press Ctrl+C or Command+C; on touch, choose
  Copy.** The cue remains immediately above the selected result and is
  included first in its focus description for assistive technology.
- Explain the platform mapping in ordinary copy near the export
  actions: Ctrl+C on Windows/Linux, Command+C on Mac, or the text
  selection menu's Copy action on a touchscreen.
- Remind readers that selecting does not copy anything and that they
  should paste into their own notes and verify the complete brief.
- Specify that **Copy brief**, rather than every Copy command, is the
  button that requests an automatic clipboard write and reports the
  browser's success response.
- Allow the cue to wrap at very narrow widths without changing other
  page layouts or increasing its visual priority.
- Update the existing Turn 108, 109, 111 and 112 regression fixtures,
  and add two Turn 114 assertions for static wording, focus
  association, responsive wrapping, asset fingerprints, and unchanged
  local-only data boundaries. Register the new regression with the
  manual release checklist.
