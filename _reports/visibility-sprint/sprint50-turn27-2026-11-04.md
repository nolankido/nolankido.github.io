## Three-goal interpretation

**Search and AI visibility:** Stable name, site identity, relevant profile destinations and source-backed deep links can make the author relationship clearer. Neither the link kit nor a sitemap update demonstrates that a search engine has indexed, ranked or cited the page. Do not distribute generic links indiscriminately.

**Competence on arrival:** The new section on `/bio/` links the peer-reviewed 2012 accounting publication and actual practical-AI projects. It does not infer a current MIT role or a professional title from article coauthorship. The public narrative clearly distinguishes tool examples, research authorship, poker reporting and self-described interests.

**AI in real life and other interests:** GitHub and DEV are led to the Technology work; Substack to the AI Works Human Decides essay; NolanPoker to the personal player/video page. All have the same canonical author home rather than unrelated keyword-heavy copies.
