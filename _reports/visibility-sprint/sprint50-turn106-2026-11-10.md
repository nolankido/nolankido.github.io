# Turn 106: Open the selected Task Brief at its first line

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `aec56af6a05478e39e2874a3686bab1a6f103900`

## Observed reader problem

In live Chrome at **320 by 700 pixels**, the Task Brief Builder's
**Select text manually** fallback selected all **1,454 characters**, focused
the output and showed the top of the textarea at **173 pixels**, just below
the **153-pixel** sticky header. The output's own internal scroll position,
however, remained at **1,265 pixels**, so the first visible line inside it
was near the end of the long selected brief. Readers had to scroll back
through their selected text to review its beginning.

## Improvement

- Immediately after focusing and selecting the full output, set the
  textarea's internal `scrollTop` to `0`. Keep the selected text range,
  copy instructions, and sticky-header clearance calculation unchanged.
- Apply the same behavior through the shared selection helper used for
  explicit manual selection and for existing clipboard, print, and download
  fallbacks. No clipboard permission or browser export promise is inferred
  from the manual-selection outcome.
- Add focused automated regression tests which execute the real helper in
  a mocked selection environment, verify the whole range remains selected,
  test conditional page correction, and confirm the behavior degrades
  safely when browser geometry APIs are missing.
- Register the new tests with the manual local release checks.
