## Reader problem and scope

The previous turn added an ordered 3–20 BB learning route to the Poker homepage.
The NolanPoker video gallery still sent viewers to narrative companions without
a visible way to continue into the matched, existing strategy-study articles.
Two Portland Meadows uploads have source-limited written companions. Other
uploads on the site lack equivalent source-checked hand records and were not

## Published change

In the public Poker gallery at `/poker/#watch`, a compact new
`#nolanpoker-hand-study` section pairs three source-documented decisions
with separate worked examples:

1. Deepstack, 6-4 offsuit near the bubble. The archived narration reports an
   approximately 148,000-chip stack and 15,000 BB, or 9.87 BB if measured
   pre-posting. Link the episode's `#hand-64o-record` to the independent
   6–10 BB guide.
2. Deepstack, pocket sevens four-handed. The narrated stack is roughly
   585,000 at 30,000 BB, or 19.5 BB if measured pre-posting. Link
   `#hand-77-record` to the 10–20 BB study framework.
3. Stand Off, A-K on the button. The reported starting stack of approximately
   61,000 at 6,000 BB gives about 10.2 BB **before** the first flat-call.
   The separate later decision followed two shoves; link the narrative's
   `#standoff-second-decision` to the 10–20 BB guide's call-price and ICM
   discussion. Do not treat 10.2 BB as the stack left at that later decision.

The existing Deepstack and Stand Off video cards each link to the new section.
The South Point, later Portland Meadows, and Reserve cards do not claim a
matching verified hand record. A separate link to the 3–5 BB guide explicitly
states there is no documented 3–5 BB example among these three hands.

The new copy says that a worked guide is **not a solver verdict** on an
individual hand. No current-cut chapter times, board runouts, payout claims,
verified ranges, or expected-value calculations were fabricated.
