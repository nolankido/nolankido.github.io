five videos despite the owner's later public South Point upload. The two
entry points also hardcoded the five-video count. That made the website a
less complete route to NolanPoker's self-published work. No YouTube audience

  **NolanPoker** and channel `https://www.youtube.com/@NolanPoker`.
  The title and channel identity were checked on November 12, 2026.

  request. The oEmbed result alone does not verify the venue, the footage,
  hand history, tournament structure or payouts.
- Added that video first in the Poker hub's public video grid and the player
  profile's viewing list. Both now accurately describe six linked videos.
- Distinguished this newer $120-tournament video from the separately
  independently listed South Point Lucky 300 result. Its video title is not
  used as proof of the result.
- Kept the Portland Meadows Deepstack and Stand Off written companions,
  caveats, routes and historic result links unchanged. The other video
  uploads remain link-only without invented timestamps or verified hands.
- Updated the Poker hub's manifest modification date and the standalone
  profile's structured-data and sitemap date to `2026-11-12` because their
  actual public content changed. Canonical URLs and existing publication
  dates are preserved.
- Added three source-and-output tests with negative controls, and extended
  the existing gallery contract to six videos. A Turn 5 predecessor test
  had an obsolete assumption that today's hub must still match an early
  release byte for byte. It now checks the exact original Turn 5 Git commit
  `f79c0a2fe71a406acf841ff0c54e805a5d46d2ed` with its pinned blob
  hashes, allowing legitimate future edits without weakening history.
