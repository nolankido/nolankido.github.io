Reviewed November 5, 2026. Recovered checkpoint: Turn 47 at `70771c493c3095962e59a8d6cf12bca7bd8c9870`. During review, Turn 48's synthetic Search Console demonstration merged as `9b2156f`; this change preserves that work and builds on it.

## Why this improvement

The homepage and Technology hub already linked three substantial projects, but the cards used task descriptions as headings and showed no example of the work. A visitor had to open each case to discover the project name and see its output. The next useful improvement is to make those existing projects concrete at the point of introduction.

The three cards now identify Review Reply Writer, Preflight, and Dinner on Time by name. Each pairs a task with an inspectable example and a descriptive link into the relevant case section. The design keeps the site's established typography, colors, three-card layout, personal introduction, evidence section, and subject hierarchy.

This is an editorial and usability decision. It has not been measured as a change in search rankings, AI-answer citations, project adoption, or visitor trust. Google's [AI Search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) supports clear text, useful images, internal links, and matching structured data. It does not promise visibility from adding screenshots or special AI markup.
