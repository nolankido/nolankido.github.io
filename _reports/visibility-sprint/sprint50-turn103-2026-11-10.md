# Turn 103: Bring the assembled brief into view

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `6bc2dad5cbfd38a3b47966b70e636faf4dc83012`

## Observed reader problem

In a real Chrome browser at **320 by 700 pixels**, a first-time visitor
could use the meeting starter and press **Assemble my brief**. All six
sections were assembled, the brief appeared, and keyboard focus correctly
moved to **Your task brief**. However, the heading's top edge was at about
**645 pixels**, near the bottom of the 700-pixel viewport. The opening
preview content was outside the visible area. The sticky header occupied
about **153 pixels** of the screen. This is an observed viewport handoff
problem, not a conversion or satisfaction measurement.

## Improvement

- Preserve semantic focus on the assembled result's heading for keyboard and
  assistive-technology users.
- After successful assembly, center that heading in the viewport with an
  immediate scroll. This allows a phone reader to see the heading, safety
  summary, and beginning of the preview together.
- The scroll is scoped to successful assembly only. Invalid required fields
  still focus the first field with an error; edits still hide stale output.
- If the browser lacks `scrollIntoView`, the established focus remains and
  assembly continues without an exception.
- Keep the existing review-before-use warning and copy, text download, print,
  and manual-select controls unchanged. No content date is advanced for a
  behavioral repair.
- Add three targeted regressions, including actual script execution against
  a synthetic DOM to verify focus, centered positioning, rebuild behavior,
  and the no-method fallback. Register the tests in the local release list.
