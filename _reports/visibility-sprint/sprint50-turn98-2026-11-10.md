# Turn 98: Pin the original release-integrity history

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `9ace5fb7eb9e4c2ca62b042cd44e7d61d130b8e3`

## Observed issue

The optional `_tests/test_turn34_release_history.py` module had nine
failing historical assertions. Its tests were feeding today's updated
website files to the Turn 34 predecessor adapter and assuming that the
adapter would recognize them as its exact November 4 release inputs.
The adapter instead safely preserved the later, unrecognized bytes.
That behavior is intentional: a hash-gated historical audit must not
silently replace later edits or arbitrary input with an old snapshot.

The stale tests also expected the four original live-download receipt
targets to have identical bytes today. Those original receipts must be
tested against their exact archived release blobs.
