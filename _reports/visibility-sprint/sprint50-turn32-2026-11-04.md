### Decision-quality distinction

The key educational and editorial improvement is separating **the original flat for 15,000** from **the later choice to invest approximately 46,000 more after two shoves**. A-K is a strong starting hand, but the two actions ask different questions. Chips already committed do not themselves prove the later call correct. Nor can the observed K-K and Q-Q determine the players' actual preflop ranges, ICM incentives, or optimal play.

The page does not quote private players' names, publish source audio files, identify spectators, assert a tournament calendar date, invent card suits for the opponents, show an unverified runout, claim certified solver equity, or give a payout figure absent from the source.
