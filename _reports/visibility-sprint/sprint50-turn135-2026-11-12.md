# Turn 135: Make the About profile's wine-and-games section directly navigable

Date: November 12, 2026

Baseline: `3aaf1b0f12a2b0dca675b805c08426a5224d048b`

## Observed gap

Turn 134 added a carefully bounded **Wine, games, and the rest of life**
section to About. Visitors could arrive there through the homepage or the
local Find directory, but the About page's own **Explore this profile**
section menu had only five links and did not expose that section. The new
content was therefore less discoverable for readers who started on About.

The live About page was inspected in a browser before editing. Its five
section links were present, each 44 pixels high. At a 390 by 844 viewport,
they appeared in a two-column arrangement with no observed document-width
overflow. These measurements are a manual browser inspection, not a user
study, assistive-technology certification, or ranking measurement.

## Improvement

- Add **Wine and games** to the native, accessible **Explore this profile**
  menu immediately after **Other interests**, linking directly to the existing
  `#wine-games-and-life` heading. The menu now has six distinct destinations.
- Preserve the About-specific responsive layout: two columns below 480 pixels,
  a 44-pixel minimum target height, wrapping and heading scroll offsets.
  The existing target outline remains available after native fragment jumps.
- Update the historical About navigation contract for the intentionally
  expanded menu and add five focused Turn 135 tests. The new tests protect
  link order, target uniqueness, source/output consistency, mobile CSS,
  direct homepage entry, canonical identity and evidence limitations. They
  also reject damaged or duplicate anchors.
- Keep the original About content, hobby caveats, document metadata,
  publication dates and canonical URLs unchanged. No Person credentials,
  private prototype names, sitewide styles, GitHub Actions, external service
  or paid tools were added.
