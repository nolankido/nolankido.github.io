4. **For actual AI-answer observations:** run the unchanged [25 prompts](../../downloads/ai-answer-sprint-25-prompts.csv) in consistently selected assistant/search modes, store displayed answers and links privately, and use the [offline audit](../../downloads/ai-answer-sprint-25-audit.py) to reject duplicates or lookalike domains. 5. **Make only an evidence-backed next change:** prioritize confirmed crawl or canonical problems, then genuine query/page mismatch or poor CTR on pages with impressions, then factual and answer clarity gaps shown by actual assistant samples. Track changes and remeasure; do not assume one month's change is caused by the site sprint. Limit the follow-up to at most two high-confidence actions per monthly readout.
