# Turn 63: Keep Nolan Kido's About and Bio navigation readable with enlarged text

Date: November 7, 2026

Baseline: `35abe16ae7d52d393db16f641b8431fafb9f80cf`

## Observed problem

The six primary links connect Nolan Kido's introduction to Technology, Living Well, Poker, Creative Work, About, and Contact. An offline Chromium audit of thirteen representative pages at a 320-pixel viewport, 200% root text and increased text spacing found navigation labels exceeding their grid cells on five pages. **About and Bio** are the highest-priority examples because they introduce Nolan by name and lead to evidence of his work and interests. On About, the Technology label had 85 pixels of available width but required 141; Living Well required 158 and Creative Work required 186. These are local layout measurements, not search or visitor analytics.

The prior turn repaired Start Here and Writing with page-scoped navigation rules. The same three-column mobile grid remained on About and Bio, where `white-space: nowrap` prevented longer labels from fitting. Their document width remained 320 pixels, concealing the label collision from an ordinary horizontal-overflow test. The separate Living Well, Resources and task-brief findings are left for subsequent scoped review.

Keyboard section navigation revealed another related problem at that size: About's **My approach** heading landed roughly 71 pixels behind the sticky header, while Bio's **More context** heading landed about 135 pixels behind it. These offsets were measured in reduced-motion Chromium after scrolling to the existing native fragment targets.

## Focused improvement

- In `assets/about-reader.css` and `assets/public-profile.css`, give only About and Bio a wrapping mobile navigation at widths of 620 pixels or less. Each of the six links remains in its original order with its original destination.
- Allow their labels to wrap naturally and give the NK mark a minimum size that grows with enlarged text. All other pages retain their current layout and the earlier Start Here and Writing safeguards.
- Give the established section headings a mobile scroll offset, so an in-page fragment jump remains readable below the enlarged sticky header. The targets, URLs and visible wording are unchanged.
- Preserve the shared navigation source unchanged. No desktop layout rule, page copy, article source, schema, identity identifier, publication date, sitemap or feed is changed.

The two page-specific stylesheets receive new content-derived cache versions. The main builder regenerates **only `about/index.html` and `bio/index.html`** to reference those new versions. Both HTML documents otherwise remain byte-for-byte identical to their previous versions.
