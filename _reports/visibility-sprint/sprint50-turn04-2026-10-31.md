## Diagnosis and change

The homepage already named Nolan Kido, showed the exact tagline **AI Works Human Decides**, and gave three inspectable practical-AI project cases prominent space. But its first-screen links went to the AI method, About, and Technology. Someone trying to connect Nolan's name with tournament poker had to pass the AI projects, selected reading, and the lower destination cards before discovering the poker route.

The Turn 4 source and published `index.html` now:

1. Preserve the name, tagline, full existing practical-AI introduction, project cases, selected reading, and all four subject destinations in their current order.
2. Add one bounded, truthful sentence: **I also build software tools and play tournament poker.**
3. Replace the generic hero action list with a semantically labeled native navigation row linking directly to three existing, published topic anchors plus About.
4. Reuse the site's responsive flex-wrap links and 44-pixel minimum hit targets without new JavaScript, visual assets, tracking, analytics, redirects, or an unverified personal achievement claim.
