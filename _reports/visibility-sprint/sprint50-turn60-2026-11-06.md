# Turn 60: Keep a completed AI task check before moving on

Date: November 6, 2026

Baseline: `dbce84138980bd3da027fe4713e1ca2941730e7d`

## Outcome

The [Should I use AI for this task? guide](https://nolankido.com/technology/should-i-use-ai/) now lets readers copy or download their completed check before following its next-step link. The record contains all six selected answers, the suggested next step, every applicable reason, the limits of the guide and the next resource's full URL.

Previously, the guide offered a suggestion but only a blank worksheet to download. Its deliberate page-memory reset cleared the six answers when a reader left for the task brief or another resource. Printing also hid the question form, leaving the suggestion without the selected answers that produced it.

This change makes the existing Decide-to-Brief path more useful: readers can keep the reasoning in their own notes and carry it into the next task. The record identifies the guide's author as Nolan Kido and links to its canonical source. It records selected answers and an editorial suggestion, not a separately recorded human decision or approval.

Direct public GETs confirmed that the guide, script and stylesheet matched their baseline repository bytes before editing. A local browser reproduced the missing copy/save controls and the enlarged-text layout issue described below.

## Reader experience

After a valid submission, **Keep your answers and suggested next step** appears before the outgoing next-step link. It provides:

- **Copy completed check**, using the clipboard only after a click.
- **Save completed check (.txt)**, requesting a file named `ai-task-check.txt`.
- **Select text manually**, opening a readable preview and selecting its complete text.
- A native **Read or copy the complete check** disclosure for inspection before saving.

Question and answer wording comes from the rendered fieldset legends and selected labels. The implementation does not maintain a second question dictionary. The visible preview, clipboard request and downloaded file use one current text record. The pause result's same-page method link and other relative destinations become absolute production URLs in the saved copy.

The existing external-action boundary and evidence limits remain in both the page and the export. The introduction now explains that resetting or leaving clears this page, while deliberately copied or downloaded files remain under the reader's control.

## State and recovery

Changing an answer, submitting an incomplete or invalid set of answers, clearing the form, leaving the page or restoring it from browser back/forward cache clears the old record and its feedback. A revision counter also prevents a delayed clipboard success or failure from changing a newer result, including a new submission with identical answers or a later manual-copy action.

When clipboard access or a download is unavailable, the current record remains readable and selectable. Temporary download links are removed and object URLs are revoked. Download feedback says the request was made and asks the reader to check the saved file; it does not claim to observe a successful save.

Browser testing found that native text selection omitted an artificial final newline. The record now ends at its final sentence, so the preview, clipboard content, downloaded bytes and selected text can agree exactly.

The implementation adds no free-text question, file upload, storage, query transport, analytics event or AI call. The site's existing privacy boundaries still apply to browser behavior and any later use of a copied file.

## Layout and printing

The baseline page fit a normal 320-pixel viewport but extended to **348 pixels** with 200% root text and expanded text spacing. The mobile navigation and long words within question fields caused the overflow. Scoped rules allow navigation, field labels, buttons and main text to wrap, and let the NK mark grow with its text. The updated page fits the same 320-pixel enlarged-text check.

The completed record uses a wrapping, scrollable plain-text preview on screen. Print rules expand the preview even when its disclosure is closed, remove the screen height limit and hide the copy/save controls. The existing complete blank worksheet remains printable independently of the optional form.
