Scope: existing hand-review guide, metadata and one new focused test

## Reader problem

The general <https://nolankido.com/poker/reviewing-a-hand/> guide offers a
fictional K-Q flop-raise example and an after-session method for separating
facts, remembered judgments, unknowns and outcomes. It did not directly link
to the three actual tournament decisions documented in the two Portland
Meadows companions, despite those records now providing original,
outcome-independent reader exercises (Turns 151–160).

A reader who completed the fictional example could reach a worksheet,
but lacked an immediate opportunity to apply the same process to
source-limited personal tournament reports. This was verified in the
authored file and public page, not inferred from traffic.

## Implementation

Inserted one small, accessible `poker-jump` navigation inside
the existing **Leave one useful next step** section, following the
method paragraph and before the blank review-sheet link.

It offers three distinct, local destinations:

1. **6-4 near the bubble**:
   `/poker/episodes/portland-meadows-ep1/#hand-64o-review`.
   The hand was narrated as a shove with about 9.87 BB and
   13 players remaining for 10 paid; exact effective stacks,
   payout implications and ranges are unresolved.
2. **Pocket sevens four-handed**:
   `/poker/episodes/portland-meadows-ep1/#hand-77-review`.
   The reported 19.5 BB reshove and later win are a prompt to
   examine prior action and alternative ranges, not proof of EV.
3. **A-K facing two shoves**:
   `/poker/episodes/portland-meadows-standoff/#standoff-second-review`.
   This is the separate call decision after the 15,000 flat, with
   approximately 46,000 chips remaining. The reported later
   showdown does not establish a solver or ICM answer.

The navigation is explicitly labeled **"Recorded hands, not solved
strategy"**. The fictional K-Q lesson, original pot ledger and
worksheet are unchanged. A short revised editorial note distinguishes
the historical October 7 improvements from the three November 16
source links. No current video timecode, new card detail, payout,
range, solver solution or result is invented.
