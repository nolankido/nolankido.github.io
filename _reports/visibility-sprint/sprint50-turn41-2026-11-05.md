# Turn 41: make NolanPoker easier to find from the Poker pages

Reviewed November 5, 2026. Starting `main` commit: `3c395223378df22f18c982daefb35c28d6142114`.

## Why this turn

The Poker hub offers a large free learning library before its five-video, first-person tournament gallery. The original intro gave learners a clear next action but did not present a comparable early route for visitors who arrived to watch Nolan's tournaments. The gallery and the standalone player profile listed the public videos without a direct action to find and subscribe to Nolan's existing YouTube channel. Although About already linked that channel, someone on the Poker page needed to infer the next step.

This is a visitor-path and identity-consistency improvement. No measurement of channel traffic, subscriptions or actual reader behavior was available, so the turn cannot claim conversion growth.

## Changes

1. The [Poker introduction](https://nolankido.com/poker/) now includes a short choice for visitors here for tournament stories: jump to the on-page video-and-companion index, or visit the existing `@NolanPoker` YouTube channel. The free beginner-course action remains first and is not gated by a signup.
2. After the [five-video gallery](https://nolankido.com/poker/#watch), a visible channel-follow route directs viewers to YouTube to subscribe. It states that written companions and independent results remain on this site. The link does **not** claim to subscribe someone automatically or add a fake subscription form.
3. The [player-profile video section](https://nolankido.com/poker/nolan-kido/#player-vlog-title) now gives the same direct channel destination alongside its existing guide and video-index links. The five known public video URLs and their factual source qualifications are unchanged.
4. A small local regression suite checks the three routes in editable source and built pages, the fixed channel handle, the existing five videos, the source-to-public byte inventory and deliberate negative controls for wrong channels, broken anchors and query parameters. The manual release-check list runs this suite without scheduling a service.
5. Earlier release-integrity tests now compare their **known historical Git blobs** while a test-only adapter recognizes precisely five reviewed Turn 41 file changes. Unexpected bytes and paths are not suppressed; no production code imports the adapter.

No new canonical URL, Article schema, Person ID, video metadata, claimed current-cut title, current timestamp, subscriber figure, private data, tracking URL, form, iframe, paid API, cron job or GitHub Actions workflow was introduced. Modification dates remain November 5 because both Poker routes were already substantively updated earlier that day.
