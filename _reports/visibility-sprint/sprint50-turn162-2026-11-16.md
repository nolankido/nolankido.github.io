# Visibility sprint Turn 162: A two-decision A-K hand-review example

Date: November 16, 2026
Base main: `c056246683f0f769534cb011978692509a138762`
Scope: One blank downloadable worksheet, one source-bounded annotated example, and an existing guide link.

## Reader problem

The published `/poker/reviewing-a-hand/` guide and its blank download
teach how to separate the situation, actions, original thinking, subsequent
review and outcome. The three real exercises introduced in Turns 156-161
reinforce missing inputs, but a reader still lacks a **completed model**
showing how to write a complex hand in that format.

The Stand Off A-K hand provides the clearest example. It has **two
different preflop decisions**: flat-calling an early-position raise,
then responding to a big-blind shove and original raiser's reshove.
Treating the roughly 61,000 initial stack as the amount faced by the
later decision, or treating the eventual K-K and Q-Q showdown as
information known beforehand, corrupts the analysis. The blank
worksheet did not explicitly require separate decision snapshots or
a source-confidence label for narrated recollections.

## Improvement implemented

### Completed, source-bounded example

Created `downloads/poker-hand-review-example-ak.md`, a seven-part
annotated review based only on the already public,
source-checked Portland Meadows Stand Off companion.

- **First decision:** A-K on the button, a reported stack around
  61,000, an early-position raise **to 15,000**, a middle-position
  call, and the actual flat-call by Nolan.
- **Second decision:** big-blind shove, original raiser's reshove,
  middle player folding, and an incremental call of roughly **46,000**
  or a fold preserving that remainder.
- **Verified arithmetic conditional on narration:**
  61,000 / 6,000 = about 10.17 big blinds before the flat;
  61,000 - 15,000 = roughly 46,000 remaining;
  original raise-to 15,000 minus the 6,000 posted live big blind
  = **9,000** additional chips for the big blind to flat, with a
  **separate** 6,000 big-blind ante.
- **Decision objectives:** distinguishes a first-in or rejam's
  possible fold equity from the later call against two all-in
  opponents. It asks for exact range, pot, coverage and payout
  assumptions rather than treating a generic push/fold chart as
  a multivariate answer.
- **Source confidence:** explicitly separates reported recollection,
  derived arithmetic, post-session reasoning and unavailable facts.
  The later hotel interpretation is not repackaged as an at-table
  written note or established opponent range.
- **Showdown after the decisions only:** reported K-K, Q-Q and
  A-K losing appear in section 6, not as known cards in either
  earlier decision snapshot. It reports the observed outcome, not a
  solver or ICM verdict. No board runout or missing suits are invented.
- **Actionable stopping rule:** if exact pot contributions, stacks
  and payouts cannot be recovered, do not assign numerical pot odds
  or a definitive recommendation.

The example has direct source links to the recorded first decision,
second-decision exercise, reported showdown and the relevant
10-20 BB chip-EV/ICM lesson. No video timecode is inferred.

### Better blank worksheet

Updated the existing `downloads/poker-hand-review.md` without turning
it into a personal-data form or a completed example.

- Collect original evidence sources and distinguish a
  contemporaneous record from later recollection.
- Label every material claim as verified record, recollection,
  arithmetic, assumption or unknown.
- Track stack conventions before/after forced contributions,
  chips behind, live bets versus dead antes, coverage, pot and
  incremental call amounts for **each separate decision**.
- Identify the decision objective and available action branches at
  the exact point being evaluated.
- Stop without a numeric conclusion when critical opponent ranges,
  pot contributions or payouts remain unresolved.
- Link to the newly annotated example.

The blank sheet still has exactly seven ordered sections and no
private personal details, opponent identifiers or stored answers.
Existing private copies are not accessed.

### Discoverability without an extra indexing dependency

Added a single **native poker-jump navigation** within the
hand-review guide's existing final study section, pointing to the
worked Markdown example. It is separate from the Turn 161 three-hand
navigation and is deliberately excluded from the Poker Find
full-text paragraph parser.

This avoids changing the indexed guide prose or requiring unrelated
large generated search indexes to be modified through a connector.
The authored guide and committed public HTML differ from the
previous release by **only the one new navigation**. The
guide remains about four minutes, with its original six H2
sections and fictional K-Q pot ledger unchanged.

The guide's original October 5 publication date and November 16
modification date remain correct. The main sitemap and
both RSS feeds are byte-identical to the baseline.

## Checks actually performed

GitHub connector-side static checks passed in separate bounded calls:

1. The completed example and blank sheet both have all seven numbered
   parts in order. The sample keeps the opponents' revealed hands
   out of Parts 1-5.
2. Its numerical examples retain 61,000 - 15,000 ≈ 46,000,
   61,000 / 6,000 ≈ 10.17 BB and 15,000 - 6,000 = 9,000
   with the live blind and ante separated.
3. The hand-review guide's authored fragment is embedded exactly
   in the public HTML. Removing the new nav produces the previous
   source and public files byte-for-byte.
4. The guide still has a six-heading outline and four-minute
   reading estimate (894 markup-stripped words, below the
   approximate 900-word threshold).
5. The manifest, sitemap, Poker RSS and writing RSS files match
   the previous release byte-for-byte.
6. The only modification to the read-only live-target registry is
   the new Markdown example; the only manual release-check
   change is one new focused unittest.
7. Original Stand Off public companion anchors were checked and
   neither companion source was modified.

**Not executed:** the full Python generator, HTML5 validator,
historical unit suite or complete browser suite. The connected
GitHub repository is readable/writable through its connector but
the container cannot clone it. The added unittest is registered
but its actual execution is not represented as passed.

No GitHub Actions, CI/CD system, cron, new paid API, data collection
or private-hand publication was introduced.
