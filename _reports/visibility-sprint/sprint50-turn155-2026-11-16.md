# Visibility sprint Turn 155: Bring documented hands into two study guides

Date: November 16, 2026 (Pacific)
Base main commit: `ee7e5b55f1ed36af809d828e5c1ade6613062497`
Scope: Two existing published tournament-study guides. No new strategy solves.

## Reader problem

The Poker hub and the two Portland Meadows companion articles now contain
navigable, source-bounded three-hand study routes (Turns 151–154). Readers who
land directly on the 6–10 BB or 10–20 BB educational guides, including
visitors from search, lacked links to the real documented decisions. The
guides already contain reproducible but *fictional* calculation fixtures;
using those to declare a winner's action correct would damage accuracy.

## Implemented

In `/poker/six-to-ten-bb-tournament-decisions/`:

- Added an accessible, native navigation card headed
  "A public hand to examine at 9.87 BB."
- Linked the actual published Portland Meadows Episode 1
  `#hand-64o-record`. The account says Nolan shoved 6-4 offsuit from the
  small blind with about 9.87 BB, was called by A-K suited, and won.
- Identified unknown calling ranges, exact effective stack and payouts.
  Explicitly separated the reported hand from the guide's fictional
  action trees and withheld any solver verdict.

In `/poker/short-stack-decisions/`:

- Added two accessible, native reading cards for separately documented
  first-person hand companions.
- The `#hand-77-record` card links to the four-handed pocket sevens
  reshove at about 19.5 BB, where the reported double-up does not
  validate the choice.
- The `#standoff-second-decision` card links to a Stand Off A-K
  situation. The ~10.2 BB figure describes the pre-flat stack;
  after calling 15,000, approximately 46,000 remained when two other
  players shoved. That second decision is not the first call repeated.
- Both cards explicitly state that the archival accounts do not
  establish ranges, payouts or a solved ICM result.

The linked first-person sources remain separate publications with their
own provenance. No new board details, card suits, opponent ranges, hand
equities, live timecodes, tournament cashes, official results, or coaching
credentials were asserted. No at-table tools, tracking, embeds, scripts,
remote data requests or paid services were added.
