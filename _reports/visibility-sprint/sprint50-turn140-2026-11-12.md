# Turn 140: Make the homepage research record identifiable and inspectable

Date: November 12, 2026

Baseline: `a130ce1a9475c1135a62cf79d7f2352dde4eb8da`

## Observed evidence gap

The homepage's first public-evidence card described a coauthored 2012
accounting paper without naming it. The linked publisher record has the
specific title, author list, journal and publication year. MIT also hosts a
public author-manuscript record. A reader could reach the publisher but could
not identify the paper or choose the manuscript from the homepage card alone.
This is a content-clarity gap, not evidence of a search ranking or engagement
problem.

## Independent source verification

- [Wiley publisher record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1475-679X.2012.00447.x):
  *The Influence of Elections on the Accounting Choices of Governmental
  Entities*, Nolan Kido, Reining Petacchi and Joseph Weber, *Journal of
  Accounting Research*, 2012, volume 50, issue 2, pages 443-476.
- [MIT institutional repository](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/9e5c8e82-34b0-4931-a8cc-6ea0ae938829):
  the matching title, authors, journal, year and an author's final manuscript.

These sources support the bibliographic facts and broad subject matter. They
do not support a current institutional role, AI competence, AI project
performance, or other biographical additions.

## Improvement

- Replace the generic homepage research-card heading with the verified,
  directly linked article title.
- Name both coauthors, the journal, year and the bounded research topic.
- Give visitors a direct MIT author-manuscript route in addition to the DOI.
- Explicitly retain the boundary that governmental-accounting scholarship
  does not establish whether Nolan's AI projects work.
- Preserve the neighboring AI and poker evidence cards, the homepage's
  canonical and metadata, the rest of the source, and all publication dates.
  No new services, client material, analytics or automation were introduced.
- Add two focused local regression checks of exact publisher and repository
  routes, bibliographic copy, proof boundaries and sibling-card preservation.
