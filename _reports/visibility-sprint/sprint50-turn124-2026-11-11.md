# Turn 124: Make the Technology evidence comparison a direct reader route

Date: November 11, 2026

Baseline: `2f74b9f9990ca0b0df540369f0a1e3d58ac48c3a`

## Observed reader-path issue

The Technology page's public `#ai-works-human-decides` method section
compared the website-verification case with the site-visibility case. The
first link went straight to its article, but the second went to
`#site-visibility-evidence`, a teaser further down the same Technology hub.
The reader then needed another link to reach the standalone article. That
extra hop was visible in the local source and generated HTML. No click logs,
Search Console data, or usability study established how often people took
this route or whether the extra step caused abandonment.

## Improvement

- Replaced the intermediate hub-teaser route with the standalone article's
  existing canonical URL, `/technology/ai-website-visibility/`.
- Reframed the two links by the distinct questions they answer: the October
  11 HTML5 defect after 347 passing tests, and the later website-visibility
  release checks plus a free local Search Console method. The comparison
  explicitly says search outcomes remain unmeasured and neither case
  establishes higher rankings.
- Preserved the existing `#site-visibility-evidence` bookmark and its
  separate hub entry for older incoming links. The earlier reciprocal case
  article links also remain in place.
- Added a focused regression with bad-route, altered release-date, and
  inflated-outcome negative controls to the manually run release checklist.

This is an editorial navigation improvement on one existing page. No new
tracking, scripts, services, author biography claims, page URLs, or content
freshness dates were introduced. No GitHub Actions or scheduled CI.
