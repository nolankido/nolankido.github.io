# Turn 105: Keep selected Task Brief text below the mobile header

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `def658359c75fae254829b18800158449ba4cb78`

## Observed reader problem

In live Chrome at **320 by 700 pixels**, assembling the fictional meeting
brief and choosing **Select text manually** focused the read-only text field
and selected all **1,454 characters**, but its top landed at **43 pixels**.
The wrapped sticky header extended to about **153 pixels**, obscuring the
beginning of the selected text. This observation also affected the shared
selection behavior used after clipboard, print, or download failures.

## Improvement

- Consolidate deliberate text selection into one `selectVisibleText` helper
  shared by the manual-select button and the existing failure fallbacks.
- Preserve the same focused field, complete text selection, copy guidance
  and click-only export behavior.
- After selection and status feedback, measure the currently visible sticky
  header and text field. When the field begins beneath the header, scroll
  the page upward just enough to leave a **20-pixel** gap after the header.
  Use an immediate scroll, not an animation.
- Do nothing when the field is already below the header. If the geometry
  or scrolling convenience is unavailable, keep selection working without
  throwing an exception.
- Add three focused tests. A synthetic DOM executes the production script
  and verifies all four selection paths, unchanged selected range,
  conditional scroll, and fallback when scrolling APIs are missing.
  Register the suite in the manual local release checklist.
