# Turn 108: Show manual-copy guidance beside selected text

Date: November 10, 2026

Baseline: `767bb60bc8646fdc59cd6a7a66e7cee2460a7209`

## Observed reader problem

On the live Task Brief Builder at **320 by 700 pixels**, manual selection
selected all **1,454 characters**, focused the textarea, and scrolled its
first line into view. The separate export status explained that selection
did not copy text, but its message sat above the textarea and was not
visible in the focused screenshot. The text field's own label was also
partly hidden by the wrapped sticky mobile header.

## Improvement

- Add a compact manual-copy instruction directly above the read-only
  textarea. It is hidden on page load and during ordinary editing.
- Show the instruction whenever a reader deliberately selects the complete
  text, including clipboard, print, and download failure fallbacks.
  Hide it again when copying succeeds, a print/download request starts,
  the brief is rebuilt, or its entries change or clear.
- Keep the original live status region. The visible cue is separate from
  the text itself, so it cannot be accidentally included in a copy of the
  assembled prompt.
- After selecting, correct the page scroll using the **label's position**
  rather than the textarea's top. This makes the label and the cue visible
  below the sticky header while preserving focus and the complete selected
  range. The previous first-line internal scroll reset remains intact.
- Add three focused regression tests for the production handlers, including
  a synthetic mobile header, every selection fallback, non-selection
  outcomes, asset fingerprints, and unchanged content date. Add them to
  the local manual release checklist.
