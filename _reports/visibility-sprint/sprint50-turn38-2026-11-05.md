# Turn 38: a source-linked credibility path on the homepage

Reviewed November 5, 2026. Site: `https://nolankido.com/`. Starting `main` commit: `ca1686a83d1ff5b426ed44d49e3a7af46ce85149`.

## Decision

The current homepage prominently says Nolan Kido and `AI Works Human Decides`, then shows AI projects, tools and interests. The About page already has a carefully sourced evidence ladder, including a publisher record for coauthored accounting research. A person arriving at the homepage, however, had to choose About to see that independent proof alongside the applied AI and poker material. The first improvement to make without inventing credentials or collecting visitor data was to show this contrast directly on Home.

Turn 38 adds **three things you can check** between the hero introduction and AI project gallery. It is an evidence path, not another claim to expertise.

## What changed

1. **Research:** a direct source link to the published 2012 paper coauthored by Nolan Kido in *Journal of Accounting Research*. Its description explicitly says that accounting scholarship does not validate the site's AI projects.
2. **Practical AI:** a link to the existing independent review-reply audit, which tests archived fictional drafts and deliberately altered controls. The card rules out client outcome and general error-rate claims.
3. **Tournament poker:** a link to the existing player profile, which distinguishes public result sources from first-person videos and reminds readers that gross cashes are not profit.

The section links back to `/about/#about-evidence-title` for full context. It reuses the site's existing three-column responsive card layout and native link focus behavior. There is no new canonical page, identity schema, claim about past employers or education, tracking script, paid API, scheduled service or GitHub Actions workflow.
