# Nolan Kido: visibility, credibility, and public identity

Reviewed: November 4, 2026. Repository: `nolankido/nolankido.github.io`.

This is a public-safe editorial and technical strategy. It contains no private analytics, confidential work, unpublished outcomes, or personal records. The earlier 50-hour sprint remains a separate private planning document.

## The decision

The site should be Nolan Kido's **canonical evidence hub**. A visitor or search system should be able to answer three questions without guessing:

1. **Who is Nolan Kido?** An identifiable person whose work combines practical uses of AI in real tasks, an operations and accounting-research background, and sustained outside interests.
2. **Why take his work seriously?** The site links to real projects, methods, checks, corrections, and independent public records. It distinguishes peer-reviewed research, project demonstrations, self-reported experience, and third-party results.
3. **Where should I go next?** A clear primary route into practical AI; secondary routes into tournament poker, writing, creative work, and everyday life; and a way to contact or follow him.

This is not a plan to manufacture reputation through large volumes of AI prose or extra structured-data claims. Search engines and assistants cannot be made to cite a person on command. Helpful public material, durable identity links, independent references, and good technical access are the levers the site can control.

## Goal 1: earn more visibility in search and AI-mediated discovery

### A. One coherent identity, many real entry points

Keep `/about/` as the canonical person page and the shared author identity. Home, Technology, article bylines, the public bio, creator profiles, and public repositories should point back to it using the same name and topical description. Keep secondary interests visible without turning every page into an unrelated keyword list.

The likely discovery paths are different:

- **Branded lookup:** `Nolan Kido`, `Nolan Kido AI`, `Nolan Kido poker`, and variations. The About page, public work, and independent records should provide a clear answer with consistent links.
- **Problem lookup:** questions such as how to check an AI-assisted website change, run a local model for a bounded task, or audit an AI-drafted review reply. A genuinely useful case may earn discovery before anyone knows Nolan's name.
- **Creator and hobby lookup:** tournament video stories, the player profile, and source-linked poker resources should introduce the person without implying poker profits or an official creator designation.
- **Research lookup:** the peer-reviewed accounting paper is a separate evidence route. It should be accurately described, not marketed as AI research.

### B. Turn the Technology hub into a small library of distinctive cases

Prioritize original, first-person, reproducible cases over broad listicles. A case should contain: real task and starting point; what AI did and did not do; artifacts or screenshots that can be shared; exact checks and negative cases; elapsed or cost evidence only when measured; what changed after review; and a final human decision. Link cases in both directions to the relevant tool, methodology, About page, and other cases. Keep canonical pages stable and record meaningful revisions.

### C. Make pages easy for people and machines to understand

Prefer crawlable, visible HTML with accurate headings, descriptive internal anchors, explicit source links, dates, stable author attribution, and honest labels such as `recorded case`, `guide`, `fictional example`, and `self-reported account`. Keep metadata and visible text aligned. Preserve the local build and search validators. Avoid unsupported rich-result promises and unnecessary new schema types.

Google's current [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [AI Search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), and [ProfilePage guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/profile-page) support clear authorship, sourced information and ordinary search fundamentals. They do not provide a shortcut guaranteeing inclusion in AI-generated answers.

### D. Distribute evidence from the places Nolan already publishes

Use substantive, platform-native summaries on Substack, DEV, GitHub, LinkedIn when approved, and YouTube descriptions or companion pages. Link to the specific canonical case rather than only to the homepage. Invite genuine references from collaborators and relevant communities when the work merits them. Avoid link schemes, bulk syndication without editorial purpose, fabricated testimonials, or automated outreach.

## Goal 2: the About page should communicate competence in the first screen

The five-second test is: name, practical AI focus, real-world judgment, and one reason to believe. A visitor who stays longer should find source-linked evidence, the boundaries of each claim, human interests, and contact routes. The strongest presentation is not a claim to be an expert in every subject.

Use an **evidence ladder**:

1. **Independent publication:** a primary publisher record and an institutional manuscript for the accounting paper. State precisely what it establishes.
2. **Inspectable work:** public case records and functioning tools, with concrete failure cases and honest limits.
3. **Independent activity records:** public tournament results where available, distinct from stories, gross cashes and personal reflections.
4. **Self-description:** background, interests, values, and ambitions clearly in the author's own voice.

The next major turn implements this ladder near the top of `/about/`, changes the generic H1 into a named profile heading, and gives the search snippet a more specific description. It preserves the existing person ID, external profiles, public links, privacy boundaries, and non-AI interests. It does not claim unverified employers, degrees, current appointments, client outcomes, testimonials, AI benchmarks, or tournament profit.

Future visual improvements can include one genuine portrait, a selective professional timeline, and a short media kit once Nolan approves the exact facts and image rights. These would probably have more human trust value than another invented credential or a large generic article batch.

## Goal 3: associate Nolan with practical AI and the rest of a real life

The primary association should be **AI in real life, where human judgment remains accountable**. The distinct editorial proposition is showing how an AI workflow works in context, including checking, repair, privacy, total effort, and when to stop. Small-business operations and personal workflows supply useful contexts, not proof of a product's success.

The secondary associations have their own authentic evidence:

- **Tournament poker:** live player perspective, study decisions, sourced results, and filmed episodes.
- **Writing and creative work:** published essays, storytelling, editing process, and project demonstrations.
- **Living Well:** attention, meaning, relationships and everyday choices that technology should serve, not consume.
- **Travel, food and learning:** occasional specific first-person stories when there is real material, not empty category expansion.

Use natural, contextual cross-links. An AI-assisted poker-video editing case can connect Technology, Creative Work and Poker, but should have one clear primary home. Similarly, a personal travel or food workflow can connect practical AI to ordinary life only if the record is real and permissions are respected.

## Measurement and decision rules

Establish a 28-day baseline, then compare another 28-day period. Keep authenticated reports outside this public repository.

| Question | Measure | Interpretation caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Are more people discovering Nolan by name? | GSC and Bing branded impressions, clicks, query variants, and About/Home landing pages | Namesakes and changing demand complicate attribution. |
| Do strangers find useful AI material? | Nonbranded practical-AI queries and qualified entry pages; engaged visits when first-party analytics supports it | Rankings and visits do not by themselves show expertise or satisfaction. |
| Are search assistants citing Nolan accurately? | A dated, repeatable manual query set across multiple services; record exact cited URLs, question wording and whether claims are correct | Answers vary by user, model and date. A citation is not a traffic or endorsement metric. |
| Does About build confidence? | Short independent five-second and one-minute reader checks: who is Nolan, what does he do, what is the evidence, what interests him? | Collect the actual answers rather than asking for a vague 1-10 polish score. |
| Does the content lead to useful action? | Relevant case clicks, tool use where observable, follow-through to contact or subscribe, and direct feedback | Avoid invasive individual tracking and do not equate page views with business results. |

The decision rule is to repeat the content formats that produce relevant discovery and genuine reader understanding. Stop publishing formats that only add volume, and preserve a record of changes rather than retroactively declaring success.

## Release rules

- Do not use GitHub Actions, another scheduled CI service, cron jobs, or paid APIs.
- Preserve established URLs, publication dates, shared Person `@id`, private boundaries, and manual publishing ownership.
- Do not publicize clients, operations, financial figures, contact records, unpublished projects, or private analytics.
- No invented employer roles, educational qualifications, institutional affiliations, testimonials, paid endorsements or performance claims.
- Search-facing descriptions must match visible content. Cite primary sources for external records.
- Run the local build, deterministic check, search and HTML validators, focused unit tests, and broader local suite where practical. Check the deployed public bytes separately after publication.

The site has enough material for authority to emerge from substance. The immediate bottleneck is showing that substance to a new visitor quickly, accurately, and in an organized way.
