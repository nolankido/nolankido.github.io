# Complete writing discovery

Baseline: production `2bae9a8abe0abd63776f6425b423939bd44b5d00`.
Implementation: October 12, 2026, in Nolan's Pacific/Honolulu timezone.

The Writing page offered selected paths and six general Notes, but no complete
author archive. An archive makes the full body of published writing easier to
discover without changing the selected introduction or inventing credentials.
Google's people-first guidance asks whether authorship is clear and links to
information about the author. Existing article bylines and the About identity
remain; this release connects that identity to an inspectable body of writing.

The existing Writing page now includes all 90 actual Articles exactly once:
6 Practical AI, 34 Living Well, 46 Poker and 4 general essays/creative pieces.
Native details controls keep the page compact. Every article remains a regular
HTML link, available without JavaScript. Original publication dates and truthful
material labels remain visible when the group is opened. Three proposed
experiments are explicitly labelled; one recorded case remains a recorded case.
The About page links to the complete archive. Counts, titles, dates and paths
are derived from existing records rather than another manually maintained list.

CollectionPage markup names the visible author and contains an ItemList whose
order, titles and destinations match the rendered archive. This is descriptive
schema, not a claim of eligibility for a Google carousel or a ranking factor.
No awards, employment, followers, results, social profiles or credentials were
invented. No new route, browser script, cookie, form or analytics event.

An independently captured pre-edit baseline records all 197 generated outputs
and the public manifest. Direct integrity tests preserve every article HTML and
all five feeds byte for byte. A bounded historical adapter removes only marked
archive additions, the scoped stylesheet, the archive's two schema fields and
the About update date. All other output hashes remain independently checked.

Validation requires the full content suite and all existing browser suites,
plus native archive expansion, keyboard navigation at four widths with
JavaScript on/off, expanded text spacing and a scoped axe scan. Hosted CI owns
browser QA; no local browser is installed or claimed. Publication requires a
successful candidate gate, GitHub Pages deployment and exact public release
bytes. The final receipt is recorded on the pull request.

Ranking and traffic impact are unmeasured. More substantial future credibility
gains depend on genuine firsthand material and actual external recognition.
This archive improves discovery and presentation of the existing work.
The domain was initially absent from the connected GSC Wizard properties, but
was already verified in the connected Google account. Registering
`sc-domain:nolankido.com` succeeded with site-owner access and an active property.
The Google URL Inspection API reported the homepage indexed with the expected
canonical and a September 29 crawl. About and Writing were unknown to Google
at inspection time. These three inspections do not describe every site URL or
constitute live fetches of this release. No sitemap was listed for this domain
property. Submit the current live sitemap after deployment; the submission
receipt belongs in the final pull-request record.

A web-performance query for September 13 through October 10 returned no rows,
and Google did not establish a settled-through boundary. This is not a measured
ranking gain or proof that no one has visited the site. The newly connected
property makes later search measurement possible.
