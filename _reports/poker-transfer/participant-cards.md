# Poker Foundations: changed-situation transfer cards

**Draft for qualified review.** Twelve fictional problems, two per skill. No monetary stakes, account, actual players or promised outcomes. This is not a validated assessment. Use a deck or paper if useful.

Read one case without searching its answer. Say or write what you know, what you are assuming, and where you are unsure. A facilitator must keep the key separate, record an independent attempt before offering any hint, and ask about prior exposure because this repository is public. Do not enter a participant's answers into this repository.

### S1-A: Flop wager after a preflop payment

In a fictional heads-up flop, you paid 5 counters preflop. On the flop you have paid zero. The other player bets 7. Both players have at least 30 counters behind at this decision, with no all-ins or side pots.

Private cards: Kc, 7h. Shared cards: Ks, 9d, 3c.

Private card names: king of clubs; seven of hearts. Shared card names: king of spades; nine of diamonds; three of clubs.
