### S1-H: Three players on the flop

This fictional hand with 1/2 blinds has three players left: you in the small blind, the big blind and the button. Each began the flop with 200 counters. You check, the big blind bets 4, the button raises to 12, and you call 12. The big blind then makes a full legal raise to 30, and the button calls 30. It is your turn again. There are no all-ins or side pots.

Private cards: Ah, Jh. Shared cards: Ac, 8d, 3s.

Private card names: ace of hearts; jack of hearts. Shared card names: ace of clubs; eight of diamonds; three of spades.

**Try without opening an answer:** How many extra counters would calling now add? Explain why your earlier call did or did not finish your decisions on this round. Can you check now, and what responses remain available?
