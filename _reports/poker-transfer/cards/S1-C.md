### S1-C: An ante and a raised pot

In a fictional six-player hand, blinds are 2/4 and each player also posts a separate ante of 1. You are the small blind and have posted those two required payments. An earlier player raises to 9 total. It is now your turn, with no intervening raise. Everyone has at least 60 counters behind; there are no all-ins or side pots.

Private cards: Jd, 8s. Shared cards: not dealt.

Private card names: jack of diamonds; eight of spades.

**Try without opening an answer:** How many new counters would calling add? Explain which of your earlier payments counts toward matching 9. Can you check here, and what are the available response families?
