**Draft for qualified review. Not part of the older T1–T6 beginner baseline. No sessions have occurred.** This supplemental protocol uses the new S1-A through S6-B cases from [participant-cards.md](participant-cards.md). The earlier [baseline protocol](../poker-beginner-baseline/protocol.md) assigns only T1–T6 and must not be used unchanged to record these new cases. Its original forms and results remain separate.

## Prerequisites

Before recruiting or administering a learning study, obtain a genuine qualified review of the [six-lesson teaching packet](../poker-teaching-review/README.md), the earlier observation kit if used, and this exact transfer packet with [review-form.md](review-form.md). Resolve material errors, pin actual commits and reviewer decisions privately, and confirm consent and accessibility needs. This repository is public, so no filled session forms, contact details, recordings or consent data belong here. These scripts, tasks and instruments are proposals, not human-reviewed or validated.

## Proposed 6–8 person allocation for a first qualitative round

Give one new case per consenting adult beginner, matched to the lesson they were asked to study. The suggested order spans the six distinct skills. It is not random assignment or a controlled comparison. Do not imply A and B have equivalent difficulty. With seven or eight adults the last two rows deliberately repeat skills 1 and 2 only, not every skill.

## One session, with assistance labeled

1. Record the release commit, participant code, device/access needs, exact case ID, lesson exposure, actual time since learning, prior exposure and consent status **in a private copy of [session-form.md](session-form.md)**. Consent details stay in a separate private location. Do not use real names in the analysis copy.
2. Invite the person to solve and explain the assigned task without opening the site solution or searching for an answer. Do not correct them mid-attempt. Record the verbatim reasoning if consent allows, otherwise a faithful observation note. Mark skipped, incomplete and explicit uncertainty, rather than manufacturing an answer.
3. Record any facilitator hint, navigation assistance, repair link or prompt repetition separately, in sequence. An assisted answer is not an independent answer. Mark whether an independent attempt occurred before help and whether any subsequent assisted attempt occurred, and record its three reasoning points separately from the original first-response labels. If the participant chooses not to continue, stop.
4. After the first explanation is recorded, use only the matching section of [facilitator-key.md](facilitator-key.md) to identify supported / error / not demonstrated for its three proposed reasoning points. An incorrect reason with a correct number remains an explanation error. If an unreviewed flaw is found, flag and stop using that item until corrected and reviewed.
5. Allow the person to view the precise repair on the public lesson and attempt an explanation again if desired. Record this as assisted, not as new independent transfer evidence. Preserve the original first-response outcome.
6. Optional later follow-up can use the participant's unused paired form only if separately consented and reviewed. **Make a new full private [session-form.md](session-form.md) copy for that case**, linked by anonymous participant code. Record all three reasoning points independently on the new form, along with exact interval, prior and new exposures, hints and case identity. An immediately repeated prompt is not retention testing. Without genuine later observations, leave all later-follow-up fields blank.

## Aggregation and limits

Copy [summary-form.md](summary-form.md) into a **private** document and record raw counts of independent, assisted, incomplete and exposed attempts by exact case. Keep first attempts and later paired-form attempts in separate tables. Count any attempt after prompt repetition or navigation help as assisted, including when no hint or repair was used. Preserve independent and assisted results separately, and do not add these overlapping phase counts as people. Preserve yes / no / unsure counts separately for exact-case, answer-key and similar-problem exposure. Include notes on confusion and whether a repair helped the learner articulate the distinction. Do not compute comparative A/B effectiveness, six-skill mastery, improvement percentages, demographic effects or seven-day retention from this small convenience sample. An empty field is *not* zero observed participants. Changes to tasks or teaching material start a separate dated round with a new commit and review decision.

The objective is to identify specific stumbling points and improve clarity, not infer profitable poker strategy. Standard play rules, including house restrictions on at-table aids, continue to apply. No wagers or accounts are needed.
