# Poker Foundations: six usable short lessons

Reviewed October 15, 2026, starting from published commit
`bf9a4ae8a4f6afb173ce309bd18a954718db1eca`.
This is a content, task and interface audit. It contains no observed learner
results or claim that the course has been validated with beginners.

## The learning gap

The course has an entrance, six ordered lessons, card construction, betting
rehearsal, worked practice and a separate checkpoint. The remaining gap is the
core lesson visit. Its first-pass panel lists article sections and several
practice destinations, so the reader must assemble the lesson before using it.
The continuation is at the bottom of the full article. Multiple-choice banks
can also let a reader recognize an answer without producing an explanation.

| Beginner task | Prior experience | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Know what to do on this visit | Choose among section links and several tools | A complete short lesson starts the existing page |
| Understand the idea before attempting it | Find the relevant full explanation | A plain explanation immediately precedes a worked model |
| Apply the idea to a new example | Leave the lesson for a practice bank | A changed situation asks for an explanation with no answer choices |
| Check the reason and repair an error | Use the separate exercise explanation | A closed native answer explains the changed case and links to its exact repair section |
| Continue without reading every reference | Reach the article's final continuation | A next-lesson button follows the short task; the sixth leads to the checkpoint |
| Keep a private practice record | Generic recall prompts in the study card | The same six changed situations appear without solutions in the blank TXT |

The worked and changed scenes are distinct examples, not Nolan's personal hands
or proposed betting recommendations. Card diagrams include accessible full names.
Answer panels use native disclosure controls and do not grade free text. The
learner compares their own explanation with explicit reasoning. The site does
not claim to know whether that explanation is correct or the learner is ready.

## Scope and organization

The six canonical lessons, their order, URLs, goals and original full articles
remain. The new short material lives in `course-lessons.json`; the existing
course renderer adds it to those pages. The full explanation stays visible below
an explicit reference heading. Original section links still work, including
repair links from existing tools. Extra practice choices move into a disclosure
after the short task. The course map explains this routine.

Native section arrivals in the lessons use immediate scrolling. This keeps a
keyboard focus scroll from competing with the next repair-link jump on a long
page. Browser checks verify the destination is visible below the fixed header.

No new public page, script, external service, account, email form or saved
progress is introduced. The existing private-use study card remains the way to
record a next task. Reading estimates still describe the complete page, so the
course explicitly distinguishes a short lesson from the full reference.

## Basis and limits

The [IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) recommends
alternating worked examples with attempts and asking learners for explanations.
These general recommendations support the design direction; they do not
establish an effect size, duration or completion rate for this poker course.
The inference here is that one available sequence reduces the assembly work
required of a novice. That needs observation with actual beginners.

Card and action mechanics were checked against the primary
[Hold'em rules](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/games/texas-holdem/) and
[hand-ranking reference](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/games/rules/hand-rankings/)
on October 15, 2026. External publishers may advertise gambling. The examples
require no outside account, wager or purchase. Range and response assumptions
are explicitly hypothetical; no frequency is inferred from a check or one reveal.

## Verification and next evidence

stronger holdings, and compute river-call prices with exact fractions. Verify
native disclosure, full-reference arrivals, all six continuations, printing,
disabled-script operation, keyboard use, narrow layouts and accessibility.
Preserve the exact 193-page predecessor through a SHA-gated change adapter rather
than refreshing old golden files. Run all release checks and verify production
bytes before describing the change as published. Executed results belong in the PR.

Before producing a paid program, observe beginners entering a lesson, explaining
its changed situation before revealing the answer, selecting a repair, and
repeating a different example after a delay. In particular, watch whether the
six-player position explanation and the distinction between a possible hand and
a frequency remain clear. Do not treat page visits, opened answers or a learner's
self-report as demonstrated transfer or willingness to pay.
